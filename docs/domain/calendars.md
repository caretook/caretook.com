# Calendars

The calendars area's rules and decisions. An orchestrator folds a landing's lasting decisions for this area in here.

✅ PRD: [`plans/appointments-2-prd.md`](../../plans/appointments-2-prd.md) (B-61, B-62, B-63; series are in [appointments](appointments.md)). Calendar items, the sharing settings, an iCal feed, reminder emails and Google Calendar, all reading one projection.

- **Code:** schema, rules and pure planners in `packages/data/src/db/schema/calendars/` (client-safe), `google/` for Google, services in `packages/data/src/services/`, procedures in `packages/api/src/calendars/`, the page in `apps/web/app/routes/app/groups/settings/calendar/`, device helpers in `lib/calendars/`.
- **One projection** (`calendarItems.ts`): `calendarItems` gives `CalendarItem`s (uid `entry:<id>` or `todo:<id>`; `CALENDAR_ITEM_KINDS` appointment or todo; app link `/todos/<id>` for a todo; all-day items carry days with an exclusive end) for `calendarWindow` (90 days back to 90 ahead). Appointments keep cancelled ones (status `cancelled`); open todos need a due day. A note, past or future, never gives an item. Default lengths: 60 min (appointment), 30 (timed todo).
- **How much leaves** (`calendarRules.ts`, `calendarText.ts`): `shareLevel({ memberConsent, groupAllowsDetails })` is `generic` unless both are true. `calendarText(item, level, timeZone)` gives summary, when, location and description: generic is "Appointment for Pat" or "Todo for Pat", `calendarWhen` and a link; details add title, place and providers. Cancelled gets a "Cancelled: " prefix. Consent is recorded with `DETAILS_CONSENT_VERSION`; bump it when the wording changes in substance.
- **Tables** (not synced; read and written through `calendars.*`): `member_calendar_setting` (user and group unique: `remindersOn`, `reminderLeads` (up to 3 of `REMINDER_LEADS`: evening before 18:00, morning of 7:00, 120, 60, 30 minutes; default evening before and 60), details consent and version, `feedTokenHash`; no row reads as the defaults), `reminder_override` (user and entry: `mute` or `extra` leads) and `care_group.details_outside_allowed` (owners' switch, default true; it **is** synced, so the card shows offline). Permissions: a member's settings and overrides are theirs alone (`OWN`, staff included); the feed hash is system-set; the Google tables are system only. Leaving or removal deletes settings, overrides and feed and disconnects Google, in its transaction.
- **`calendarSettingService`** (`get`, `update`, `setDetailsAllowed` (owners), `removeForMember`, the unsubscribe token) and **`calendars.*`**: `settings.get|update`, `group.setDetailsAllowed`, `feed.create|rotate|revoke`, `google.status|disconnect`, `overrides.get|set` . Turning details on records consent, off clears it; a change that can alter a push queues one.
- **The settings page** (`appUrls.calendarSettings`, a group-settings subpage "Calendar & reminders"; shared route, `clientLoader`, online only for writes). Four cards, each in its own file taking `CalendarCardProps`: Reminders ("Emails before appointments and todos in this group…", `REMINDERS_EXPLAINED`), Calendar feed, Google Calendar (not on native), Details outside Caretook. Posting cards go through `CARD_ACTIONS` (`cardActions.ts`) by `intent`. A member's own settings save at once through `useCalendarSave` and `CalendarSaveLine` (`saveCalendarSettings`): the last copy is cached per user and group on the device, an offline change stays pending and the loader sends it when the connection returns; sign-out forgets both. Details card: the member's consent text and switch (disabled with a note when owners forbid). The owners' switch is on group settings → Details (`DetailsOutsideCard`).
- **The feed** (`calendarFeedService`, `renderIcs` with `ical-generator` and golden files, `GET /api/calendar/:token.ics`, web only). One feed per member and group (`create` refuses a second, 409); the token is 32 random bytes shown once and stored only as its SHA-256; `rotate` and `revoke` change it. `resolve` rechecks on every request that the member is still in the group and enabled, and reads as them at their share level. Unknown, revoked or malformed: a bodyless 404; other methods 405; 60 per minute per IP and 30 per minute per token hash, then 429. The token is never logged; responses are `private`.
- **Reminders** (`reminderPlan.ts`, pure, also used by the "Remind me" sheet; `sweepReminders` in `@caretook/notifications`, the cron's first step; see [notifications](notifications.md)). `reminderPlan` covers appointments and todos. Appointments go to members with reminders on at their leads plus an override's extras (minus a mute); a todo (notification `todoDue`) goes only to its assignee, who needs reminders on, the morning of its due day or 1 h before its time (opt-in; `TODO_LEADS`). Leads are in the **recipient's zone**. All-day items take only the two day leads. Nothing for cancelled or started items or a send already past when the item was made. The key is (recipient, uid, lead, start): a moved visit sends again, a repeat run sends nothing. The sweep looks **48 h ahead**, catches up at most 6 h, and locks `calendars.lastReminderRunAt` in `app_setting`. Wording is `calendarText` at the member's level. Mail carries `List-Unsubscribe`: an HMAC token under `linkSubkey(purpose)` of `INVITE_LINK_KEY` (without the key the sweep returns `noKey`), `/api/reminders/unsubscribe/:token`: GET shows a button, POST (also RFC 8058 one-click) turns that member's reminders off in that group.
- **"Remind me"** (per appointment; `reminderOverrideService`: `get`, `set` (`default` deletes the row), `mute`, `extra`; errors `:notFound` 404, `:invalid` 400): `RemindMeSheet`, `useRemindMe`, `lib/calendars/remindMe.ts` (`canRemind`, `remindSummary`, which lists passed leads too), before Print in `entryActionItems` for upcoming, uncancelled, unmoved events only, every role. **A member with reminders off gets nothing, extras included** (the user's decision); the sheet says so and points to Calendar & reminders.
- **Google Calendar, the client** (`google/client.ts`): the one seam. `real.ts` is over `@googleapis/calendar` and `google-auth-library`; `fake.ts` is in memory (`user.move|delete|create`, `revokeAccess`, `expireSyncTokens`, `failCalls`) for tests. Methods return an `AppError` (`googleCalendar:*`), never throw, and carry no token. Scope **`calendar.app.created`** only: our own secondary calendar, never the member's others. Events are in "Caretook: <recipient>", ids ours (base32hex, stable per item), with `caretookUid` and `caretookStatus` in private properties. Env: `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`, `GOOGLE_TOKEN_KEY` ([ops](../../ops/runbook.md) 5a).
- **Google, connect and tables** (`googleConnectionService`; `googleCalendar.ts`; migration `0029_appointments_calendars`): `google_calendar_connection` (one live per member and group; the refresh token sealed with `utils/seal.ts`, AES-256-GCM under `GOOGLE_TOKEN_KEY`, the connection id as associated data, a fixed dev key in dev and tests), `google_event_link` (item to event, `pushedHash`, etag), `calendar_sync_job` (`reason`: entry, todo, series, consent, connect, daily), `google_oauth_state` (hashed state and sealed PKCE verifier, 10 minutes, single use). `/api/google/connect` is a plain GET link (so `form-action` doesn't block the redirect) and `/api/google/callback` returns to the page with `?google=<outcome>`; viewers connect too. Disconnect revokes at Google, forgets the token, and leaves the calendar there. Account deletion doesn't revoke yet (no deletion flow). A refused token shows "Reconnect Google"; rotating the key makes everyone reconnect.
- **Push** (`calendarSyncService`, `pushPlan.ts`): a write that can change an item queues a job in its own transaction (`enqueue`, only with a live connection; `withTransaction`'s `afterCommit` starts it via `waitUntil`); the cron's `googlePush` step runs what is left (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, backoff) and `sweep` queues a daily push so items leave the window. A run diffs the items at the member's level against each link's hash: unchanged makes no call; it writes, deletes what left or was deleted. Cancelled appointments stay as "Cancelled: ..." and show free. Provider link changes queue nothing (the daily push catches them). The feed, push (enqueue `kind = 'event'`) and sweep select events (and todos) only; a Google event pushed earlier for a note isn't cleaned up.
- **Pull** (`calendarPullService`, `pullPlan.ts`: `interpretGoogleChange(event, link, item, member, now, timeZone)`; cron `googlePull`): each live connection at most every 5 minutes (`pullDue`, SKIP LOCKED; `lastPulledAt` is when a pull last started), from the stored sync token (everything when none or Google asks for a full sync). Only **times** come back; a title, place or description edited in Google is ignored. Appointments: a time change updates start, end and all-day, a delete cancels ("Deleted in Google Calendar"); a series visit changes as "Only this visit"; a started visit is never rewritten. A todo's due day and time move, a delete marks it done; a new Google event in the window becomes an appointment titled by Google's summary, which the next push tags. Actions run **as the connecting member**, so RBAC and audits are the usual. **Overwrite:** what their role can't do (a viewer's edit; their new event), or a service refuses, clears the link's hash and queues a push so Caretook's version returns. A passing failure keeps the sync token. Our own echo (stored etag, or matching times) is ignored.
- **Smoke test** (`packages/data/scripts/googleSmoke.ts`, `pnpm calendar:google-smoke`, `--keep`, `--disconnect`; manual, never in `pnpm check`; runbook 5a.6): connects, pushes, edits through Google's API, pulls, cleans up.
- **Tests:** unit for the rules, text, items, plans and golden `.ics`; integration for services, feed, procedures and the sweep (`reminders.int.test.ts`); component tests for the page and cards (`settings/calendarReminders`, `feedCard`, `plannerRemind`).
