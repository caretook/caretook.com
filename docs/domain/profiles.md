# Care recipient profiles

The care recipient profiles area's rules and decisions, moved out of AGENTS.md (whose core rules still apply). An orchestrator folds a landing's lasting decisions for this area in here.

PRD: [`plans/profiles-prd.md`](../../plans/profiles-prd.md).

- **Visibility** (`db/schema/profileVisibility.ts`, pure, client-safe; screens, services and tests use it): name, preferred name, birthday, basics and the note for helpers are read by every member and edited by writers; the recipient's phones, email, address, insurance and outside contacts are writers only (outside contacts edited by owners and admins); emergency rank and primary contact are read by every member and set by owners and admins; a member's account contact details are theirs alone, and what they share is per group.
- **Tables** (migration `0017_profiles`, which ends with a hand-written `ALTER PUBLICATION`: keep it when regenerating):
  - `care_recipient` + `code_status` (`fullCode | dnr | dnrPolst`), `blood_type` (8 values; both with DB checks), `primary_language`, `pronouns`; `notes` is the note for helpers (1000 chars) and syncs.
  - `care_recipient_details` (id = the recipient's, unique `group_id`, cascading composite FK): `phones` (≤ 6, the providers' shape), `email`, `address`, `insurance` (≤ 6; insurer and member ID required; at most one primary and one secondary), `outside_contacts` (≤ 10; name and phone required; `emergencyRank` only through the emergency order).
  - `care_group_member` + `relationship`, `shared_fields` (`phones | email | address`, default none), `emergency_rank`, `is_primary_contact` (one per group, partial unique index), `emergency_dropped_at` (system only; set when a member drops off the list, cleared by the next `setOrder`).
  - `user_contact` (phones, `contact_email`, address): the user only, **never published**.
  - `member_contact_share` (id = the membership's; composite FK to `care_group_member`, cascading, so leaving deletes it): only the shared values, rebuilt by the services in the same transaction as any contact, sharing or membership change; a row exists only while it shows something. **Co-members' contact details come only from here, never from `user` rows** (so B-83 can restrict those).
- **Services:** `careRecipientService.updateProfile` (writers; the time zone too), `careRecipientDetailsService` (`get`, `update`, `updateOutsideContacts`), `emergencyContactService` (`setOrder`, `setPrimary`; members must share a phone, else `noPhone` 409; `renumber`, `dropMember`), `userContactService`, `memberSharingService`. A member left with no shared phone loses rank and primary (and the order renumbers); membership removal leaves a gap until the next renumber, so display sorts by rank. `updateMySharing` returns `droppedFromEmergencyList`, `me.updateContact` `droppedFromEmergencyLists`.
- **oRPC** (`packages/api/src/profiles.ts`): `careGroup.recipient`/`updateRecipient` (with the basics, note and time zone), `recipientDetails`, `updateRecipientDetails`, `updateOutsideContacts`, `setEmergencyContacts`, `setPrimaryContact`, `updateMySharing`; `me.contact`, `me.updateContact`. Nothing returns a sign-in email. **Every profile write is online only**; none of these tables are in the upload switch.
- **Screens:**
  - **The profile** (`routes/app/groups/profile/`, `appUrls.profile(groupId, sheet?)`, `PROFILE_SHEETS`): not a section; reached from "<Name>'s profile" in the group menu (before Group settings) and the timeline header's `RecipientCard` (name as the `h1`, "<group> · Age N · <code status>"). Header with birthday and age (`lib/recipientDisplay.ts`: `ageText` on the recipient's day in their zone), Basics, the note, then writers' Contact, Insurance and Emergency contacts cards (`DetailsCards.tsx`, `EmergencyCard.tsx`, on `ProfileCard`). Viewers get "More details are visible to caretakers." Edit is a link only while PowerSync is connected (`useOnline` in `profileSheet.ts`), else a disabled button with "Needs a connection to edit."
  - **Sheets** `profile/basics`, `contact`, `insurance`, `emergency` (children of the profile; `profileSheet.ts`, `detailsSheet.ts`, `SheetButtons.tsx`): each saves through oRPC, then waits for the change to sync back before closing. Guards send anyone without the role to the profile. The emergency sheet (owners and admins): Move up and Move down (announced, focus kept), members sharing a phone, outside contacts in place, the primary as a `ChoiceGroup`; pure rules in `lib/emergencyList.ts` (`emergencyEntries`).
  - **Recent vital averages** (`VitalAveragesCard`, `data-section="vital-averages"`, every role, after the note; B-100): blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, weight, SpO₂ and blood sugar (`AVERAGE_VITALS`), each with the average and reading count over the last 7 and 30 days (`AVERAGE_WINDOWS`; a window starts at midnight in the recipient's zone and ends now), "—" for an empty window, in the account's units. The pure `vitalAverages()` (`lib/vitalAverages.ts`) over `recentReadingsQuery` (`lib/sync/vitalAverages.ts`, `json_each`, deleted entries excluded); the card is left out when none of the six has a reading in 30 days.
  - **The emergency sheet** (`profile/print`, `appUrls.profilePrint`; a page, writers only): the pure `emergencySheet(...)` builder (`profile/print/emergencyPrintSheet.ts`; section order in `EMERGENCY_SECTION_ORDER`, empty sections left out; allergies after medications, from `loadAllergies`/`useAllergies`, each worded by `allergyText` as `PrintAllergies` does, most severe first, severe in bold, or "No known allergies" / "Allergies not recorded" when none are listed (B-112)), from local data, on the medications sheet's print CSS. One Letter page for the seeded profile.
  - **Account settings → Contact details** (`settings/contact`, last in `ACCOUNT_SETTINGS`): phones, contact email ("Your sign-in email stays private unless you enter it here"), address, through `me.updateContact`; offline a notice and no form (not synced).
  - **Members page:** each member's relationship and shared details (`components/contact/ContactLines`, with call, text, email and map actions; `lib/contactActions.ts` has a `text` kind), Emergency contact and Primary contact badges, and on your own row "What this group sees" (`SharingForm.tsx`: checkboxes only for filled fields, none ticked by default) and your relationship. `PhoneFields` and phone parsing (`lib/contactForm.ts`) are shared with the provider form.
- **Seeds** (`seeds/profiles.ts`, own ids, appended last): Grandma Pat's basics, note, details (home phone, email, address, Medicare and a Medigap plan), outside contact Rosa Example (rank 3); member@ shares phones and email ("Son", rank 1, primary), caretaker@ a phone ("Aide from Sunrise Home Care", rank 2).
- **Tests:** `profiles.mjs` was moved (B-121). Component tests share `profile/profileWorld.tsx` (Grandma June, members and an outside contact, and a stand-in server that syncs each save back): the page, sheets, roles and offline (`profile.browser.test.tsx`; `connected: true` or `device.connect()` for the online-only Edit), emergency contacts (`emergency.browser.test.tsx`) and the print sheet (`print/emergencySheetPage.browser.test.tsx`, `commands.printMedia`). `app-shell` step 4 keeps the details stream: a demoted viewer's device loses `care_recipient_details`, and gets it back on promotion. `profiles-contacts.mjs` was moved too (B-122): the contact page (`settings/contact/contact.browser.test.tsx`) and sharing on Members (`members/memberSharing.browser.test.tsx`); `app-shell` step 4 keeps a member's share row reaching the owner's device and leaving on removal. `app-shell.mjs` re-adds members through SQL, which loses their share rows: checks that need seeded shares set up their own.
