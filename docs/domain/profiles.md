# Care recipient profiles

The care recipient profiles area's rules and decisions. An orchestrator folds a landing's lasting decisions for this area in here.

PRD: [`plans/profiles-prd.md`](../../plans/profiles-prd.md).

- **Visibility** (pure, client-safe `db/schema/profiles/profileVisibility.ts`, used everywhere): name, preferred name, birthday, basics and the note for helpers are read by every member and edited by writers; the recipient's phones, email, address, insurance and outside contacts are writers only (outside contacts edited by owners and admins); emergency rank and primary contact are read by every member and set by owners and admins; a member's account contact details are theirs alone, and what they share is per group.
- **Tables** (migration `0017_profiles`, which ends with a hand-written `ALTER PUBLICATION`: keep it when regenerating):
  - `care_recipient` + `code_status` (`fullCode | dnr | dnrPolst`), `blood_type` (8 values; both with DB checks), `primary_language`, `pronouns`; `notes` is the note for helpers (1000 chars) and syncs.
  - `care_recipient_details` (id = the recipient's, unique `group_id`, cascading composite FK): `phones` (≤ 6), `email`, `address`, `insurance` (≤ 6; insurer and member ID required; at most one primary and one secondary), `outside_contacts` (≤ 10; name and phone required; `emergencyRank` only through the emergency order).
  - `care_group_member` + `relationship`, `shared_fields` (`phones | email | address`, default none), `emergency_rank`, `is_primary_contact` (one per group, partial unique index), `emergency_dropped_at` (system only; set when a member drops off the list, cleared by the next `setOrder`).
  - `user_contact` (phones, `contact_email`, address): the user only, **never published**.
  - `member_contact_share` (id = the membership's; composite FK to `care_group_member`, cascading, so leaving deletes it): only the shared values, rebuilt by the services in the same transaction as any contact, sharing or membership change; a row exists only while it shows something. **Co-members' contact details come only from here, never from `user` rows**.
- **Services:** `careRecipientService.updateProfile` (writers; the time zone too), `careRecipientDetailsService` (`get`, `update`, `updateOutsideContacts`), `emergencyContactService` (`setOrder`, `setPrimary`; members must share a phone, else `noPhone` 409; `renumber`, `dropMember`), `userContactService`, `memberSharingService`. A member left with no shared phone loses rank and primary (the order renumbers); removal leaves a gap, so display sorts by rank. `updateMySharing` returns `droppedFromEmergencyList`, `me.updateContact` `droppedFromEmergencyLists`.
- **Conditions** (`recipient_condition`, `db/schema/profiles/recipientCondition.ts`; synced and written offline, unlike the rest of this area): `name` (≤ 200), `status` (`active | resolved`), optional `since_year` and `since_month` (a month needs a year), `note` (≤ 300), `diagnosed_by_id` (a provider in the group, `NO ACTION`), soft delete; permissions as `recipient_allergy`. `care_recipient.no_known_conditions_at`: adding a live condition clears it, setting it with live ones on file is refused (`HAS_CONDITIONS_MESSAGE`). Link tables `condition_manager` (↔ provider) and `medication_condition` (↔ medication): id a UUID v5 of the pair (`uuidV5.ts`), composite FKs cascading, hard delete on unlink. Links go only to live rows in the group (a deleted provider or condition is refused, another group's id is `unauthorized`).
  - **Services:** `recipientConditionService` (conditions, `setNoKnownConditions`, `linkManager`, `unlinkManager`, `setManagers`, `listManagers`) and `medicationConditionService`. The device writes locally (`lib/profiles/conditions.sync.ts` and its `conditionProviders`, `medicationConditions` siblings) and uploads onto the same services ([sync](sync.md)).
  - **Change entries:** each write records one `conditionChange` timeline entry in its transaction (pure `conditionChange(before, after)`, `db/schema/profiles/conditionChange.ts`, for both writers; [timeline](timeline.md)): `change` is `added | changed | resolved | reactivated`, details carry `conditionId`, `conditionName` and, for `changed`, `fields` with `before` and `after` (name, status, since, note, diagnosed by, managed by). Its id is `conditionChangeId` from the condition id and `updated_at`, so both writers agree; upload passes `updated_at` as `changedAt`. One manager save is one entry (`withManagersChange`). Delete writes none.
  - **Suggestions** (pure `conditionSuggestions.ts`): the static plain-language list in `conditionNames.ts` (about 150, common in older adults) plus the group's own names first, every typed word matched, de-duplicated case-insensitively, at most 20. Free text is always allowed; nothing is fetched.
  - **Section, sheets:** `ConditionsSection`, every member: active conditions, then Past, or "No known conditions" / "Conditions not recorded" (`conditionState`); writers get Add and "No known conditions" (the route's `clientAction`). Sheets `profile/conditions/new`, `:conditionId` (view) and `:conditionId/edit` (`appUrls.newCondition`, `condition`, `editCondition`): `ConditionForm` with `ProviderPickerField` for who diagnosed it and `ProvidersPickerField` for who manages it ([components](components.md); a deleted provider shows "(removed)"); the view lists its live medications (`ConditionMedications`). A sheet opened from another screen passes `PROFILE_SHEET_STATE` (`lib/profiles/profileSheetLink.ts`), so closing returns there.
- **oRPC** (`packages/api/src/profiles/profiles.ts`): `careGroup.recipient`/`updateRecipient` (with the basics, note and time zone), `recipientDetails`, `updateRecipientDetails`, `updateOutsideContacts`, `setEmergencyContacts`, `setPrimaryContact`, `updateMySharing`; `me.contact`, `me.updateContact`. Nothing returns a sign-in email. **Every profile write is online only** (conditions excepted, below); none of these tables are in the upload switch.
- **Screens:**
  - **The profile** (`routes/app/groups/profile/`, `appUrls.profile(groupId, sheet?)`, `PROFILE_SHEETS`): reached from "<Name>'s profile" in the group menu (age and code status only here and on the emergency sheet). Banded: `ProfileHeader` (a `DetailHeader`: avatar, name, birthday and age from `recipientDisplay.ts`, Print and Edit), then Basics (its sheet shows the time zone, linked to settings for `editRecipient`), Conditions, the note, vital averages, and writers' Contact, Insurance and Emergency contacts; a viewer's last band, "more details". Edit links only while online (`useOnline`), else "Needs a connection to edit."
  - **Sheets** `profile/basics`, `contact`, `insurance`, `emergency` (children of the profile): each saves through oRPC, then waits for it to sync back. Guards send others back. The emergency sheet (owners and admins): Move up and Move down, members sharing a phone, outside contacts, the primary as a `ChoiceGroup`; rules in `lib/profiles/emergencyList.ts` (`emergencyEntries`).
  - **Recent vital averages** (`VitalAveragesSection`, `data-section="vital-averages"`, every role): blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, weight, SpO₂ and blood sugar (`AVERAGE_VITALS`), each with the average and reading count over the last 7 and 30 days (`AVERAGE_WINDOWS`; a window starts at midnight in the recipient's zone and ends now), "—" for an empty window, in the account's units. The pure `vitalAverages()` (`lib/profiles/vitalAverages.ts`) over `recentReadingsQuery` (`vitalAverages.sync.ts`, deleted entries excluded); left out when none of the six has a reading in 30 days.
  - **The emergency sheet** (`profile/print`, `appUrls.profilePrint`; a page, writers only): the pure `emergencySheet(...)` builder (`emergencyPrintSheet.ts`; section order in `EMERGENCY_SECTION_ORDER`, empty sections left out; allergies after medications, from `loadAllergies`/`useAllergies`, worded by `allergyText`, most severe first, severe in bold, or "No known allergies" / "Allergies not recorded" when none are listed (B-112)); Conditions follow Allergies, active ones only, worded by `conditionText`, or "No known conditions", from local data, on the medications sheet's print CSS.
  - **Account settings → Contact details** (`settings/contact`, last in `ACCOUNT_SETTINGS`): phones, contact email, address, through `me.updateContact`; offline, a notice and no form.
  - **Members page:** each member's relationship and shared details (`components/contact/ContactLines`, with call, text, email and map actions), Emergency contact and Primary contact badges, and on your own row "What this group sees" (`SharingForm.tsx`: checkboxes only for filled fields, none ticked) and your relationship. `PhoneFields` and phone parsing (`lib/core/contactForm.ts`) are shared with the provider form.
- **Seeds** (`seeds/conditions.ts`, `conditionManagers.ts`, `medicationConditions.ts`: Grandma Pat's four conditions (one resolved), linked to seeded providers and medications; `seeds/profiles.ts`, own ids, appended last): Grandma Pat's basics, note, details, outside contact Rosa Example (rank 3); member@ shares phones and email ("Son", rank 1, primary), caretaker@ a phone (rank 2).
- **Tests:** component tests share `profileWorld.tsx` (Grandma June, members, a stand-in server syncing saves back): the page, sheets, roles and offline (`profile.browser.test.tsx`; `connected: true` for the online-only Edit), emergency contacts (`emergency.browser.test.tsx`) and the print sheet (`print/emergencySheetPage.browser.test.tsx`). `app-shell` step 4 keeps the details stream: a demoted viewer's device loses `care_recipient_details`, regained on promotion. The contact page (`settings/contact/contact.browser.test.tsx`) and sharing on Members (`members/memberSharing.browser.test.tsx`); `app-shell` step 4 keeps a member's share row reaching the owner's device and leaving on removal. Conditions: `conditions.browser.test.tsx`, `conditionProviders.browser.test.tsx`, and `syncConditions`, `syncConditionManagers`, `syncMedicationConditions` integration tests in `packages/api`. The `app-shell` check re-adds members by SQL, losing their share rows: checks needing seeded shares make their own.
