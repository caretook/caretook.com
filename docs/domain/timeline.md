# Timeline entries

The timeline area's rules and decisions.

- **Table:** one `timeline_entry` table with a `kind` column.
  - `group_id` and `recipient_id` are on the row (streams filter on them); the composite FK to `care_recipient` is `NO ACTION`, so deleting a recipient with entries fails, while deleting the group cascades.
  - `occurred_at` plus the device's IANA `time_zone`; optional `title`, `body`, and `details` jsonb (a Zod union per kind).
- **Rules:**
  - **Soft delete** (`deleted_at`), checked against the delete permission. Reads hide deleted rows unless `includeDeleted`, and `update` refuses them. A hard delete cascades to the entry's document.
  - **Keyset pagination** on `(occurred_at, id)`, because offline devices insert older entries.
  - `create` defaults `recipientId` and checks the group first, so a non-member learns nothing. `update` validates the **merged** row.
  - `occurredAt` reads back as text; normalise it at the API.
- **Kinds.** Place a proposed new thing to log with these first:
  - **Note:** free text about the day. **Its text is its live document**: `body` is always null, the title optional. Written at `/entries/:id`.
  - **Symptom:** something the recipient **feels or reports**: free-text `name` plus `severity` 0-10. **Pain is a symptom.**
  - **Vital:** something **measured, counted or observed**, with a fixed unit or scale (without one, like mood, it's a symptom). Bowel movement, urine output and sleep are vitals.
  - **Dose:** a medication **given, skipped or refused** (`details` has `medicationId` and `status`; no title or body). The medication must be in the entry's group; deleting it leaves its doses.
  - **Medication change:** started, changed, stopped, resumed; **written automatically** (`SYSTEM_WRITTEN_KINDS`), never created or edited by hand, though writers may delete one.
  - **Event:** something that happened. Like a note, **its text is its live document**, written at `/entries/:id`. A title is required: the device saves `"Event"` (`DEFAULT_EVENT_TITLE`) when none is given and the field shows empty with "Add a title" (`isUnnamedEvent`). A future event is an appointment (_Appointments_).
- **Vitals** (`db/schema/timeline/vitals.ts`, the single spec):
  - `details = { readings: [...] }`, at most one per type (types in `VITAL_TYPES`; blood pressure needs diastolic < systolic).
  - **Adding a type is spec-only:** `VITAL_TYPES`, `VITALS` and one `reading(...)` line (new keys in `VitalFieldSpec["key"]`).
  - Stored in canonical units (°C, kg, mg/dL, mL); display and entry use the user's units (`fromDisplay`/`toDisplay`).
  - Hard limits reject; outside the typical range warns but saves. `isKnownReading()` skips unknown types.
- **User preferences:** `user.preferences` jsonb (`userPreferencesZSchema`, every field optional, so new settings need no migration): `units`, `timelineView` (`rows | cards`), `daySummary` (`off | highlights | full`, default `highlights`).
- **Timeline screen** (`/app/groups/:groupId/timeline`, `routes/app/groups/timeline/`):
  - Rows or cards, both from `entrySummary()`. `KIND_META` holds kind icons and colors; vitals are pink, **not green** ("all good"). Day headers (`DayHeader`: the date `text-lg lg:text-xl`, the + menu, the period line, and the day summary in its `summary` region, sticky, on `day-band`) use the pure `dayLabel` (schema `core/`). A row's ⋯ and its author chip stack in a right column (`[data-entry-aside]`, `ASIDE_CLASS`); `ViaTag` sits in the footer.
  - **Filters live in the URL** (`?kind=&author=<userId>`, and `?vital=<type>` only under `kind=vital`; changing the kind drops it), owned by the pure `lib/timeline/timelineFilters.ts`. **Every filter control is a `Link` built through it** (`replace`, `preventScrollReset`). A filter change starts from one page. **One Filter · N button** opens `FilterSheet` (Kind; one vital type under Vitals; the period while searching; Clear keeps `q`); each filter on is a removable chip under it ("Filters on").
  - **Kind icon** and **author control** (`components/entry/KindIcon.tsx`) are links ("Show only <kind plural>", "Show only <name>'s entries") above the row's open target, for viewers too. An author filter adds a "By <name>" chip clearing only the author (a former member still filters).
  - **The Today card** (`TodayCard`, full view only, every width; ignores filters): one row each for the latest vitals, the next appointment, medications due (Dose sheet link; Given all for writers) and overdue or due-today tasks (mine first), each hidden when empty, ending in "See all ›" but vitals, which wrap in place behind "Show all" on overflow (`TodayRow` `expandable`). The pure `todayStatus` picks a row's hue, always with an icon and screen-reader text: `error` an overdue dose or task, `warning` a dose due now or vitals over 24 h old, `info` an appointment in progress, today or within 2 h. **Collapsed on every load** (a tap opens it) to `todaySummary`'s line, led by `attentionCounts` (never truncated): red "N overdue", orange "N due soon" (doses, tasks or the next appointment due now or within 1 h; an untimed task is due 19:00 recipient time), then the rest. `LiveNowBanner` sits beside it (who is writing, with **Join**; rows show a `LiveDot`).
  - **Tiles never pop in:** the Today card's rows and simple mode's blocks read through the tile cache (sync.md): a remembered answer renders dimmed (`data-stale`) with actions off until fresh, nothing remembered a fixed-height skeleton.
  - **Wide layout:** from `lg` the header cards and entries stop at `STRIP_CAP`, `COLUMN_CAP` (`timeline/layout.ts`). **Cards stay one column at every width** (side-by-side breaks the time order).
  - **Adding** (`QuickAddMenu`): a + button "Add to <day label>" beside each day's heading; writers only, not in simple mode. Another day's adds carry `?at=` (`START_AT_PARAM`), set by `dayStartAt`: 12:00 that day in the viewer's zone.
  - **Sheets.** Note and Event open `/entries/new?kind=…`. Symptom and Vitals open the **add-sheet route** `timeline/new/:kind` (`symptom | vital`, `?type=<vitalType>`; `appUrls.newSheet`) in the timeline's `Outlet`, guarded by `newSheetGuard`. **Editing** is `timeline/:entryId/edit` (`appUrls.editSheet`); `editSheetGuard` sends a note or event to its page, anything else uneditable to the timeline. `useCloseSheet` goes back or replaces, so Back never reopens a saved sheet. **Sheet links use `prefetch="render"`** (offline after a full load).
  - **Row taps** (`EntryOpenTarget`, one stretched link per row): Notes and events open `/entries/:id` for everyone; symptoms and vitals open their edit sheet for writers and nothing for viewers (`openRule`). ⋯ has Delete and Edit (writers) and **Print** (notes and events, everyone).
  - **The quick-entry box** (writers): `components/quick-entry/`, rules in [assistant](assistant.md).
  - **Day summaries** (`DaySummaryLine`, one truncated line in its `DayHeader`, tap to expand; pure `daySummary.ts`): vitals (a range with an arrow), symptoms (highest severity, "(new)" against the baseline), events, who logged. **No note count and no doses**; a day with no vitals, symptoms or events gets no line. **Highlights** are vitals with an arrow, new or severe (`SEVERE_SYMPTOM`) symptoms, and events. The viewer's `daySummary` (the header's `TimelineViewMenu`) picks the collapsed line; expanded is always the full breakdown. **Any kind, author, vital or search filter hides every summary** (`summariesOff`).
  - **The history ends at now** (`historyWindow`).
  - **Infinite scroll:** `OlderEntries` calls `loadOlder`; the limit grows by `PAGE_SIZE` (`lib/core/paging.ts`). The previous window shows until a larger one fully arrives (`useLiveRows`). Back from an entry page restores it (`timelinePaging.ts`: same `location.key` and filters, ≤ 5 pages). Older pages' rows get `content-visibility: auto` (`offscreenRows.ts`); the newest stays uncontained, so paging doesn't shift. No virtualising.
  - **The route only composes** (`route.tsx`): data from `useTimelineData`, the view from `useTimelineView`, deletes from `useEntryRemoval`. The loader and `meta` share `pageTitle()` (one chunk).
- **Seeds** (`seeds/timelineEntries.ts`):
  - **Ids follow position**, so a change needs `pnpm datastore:reset`; `timelineEntries.test.ts` validates each against the content schema (seeds bypass the service).
  - A note's or event's seed `body` becomes its document (`seedContentOf`); the entry's `body` stays null.
- **Tests:** component tests beside the route (`timelineWorld.tsx`); browser checks only for the server.
- **The simple view** (a view of the timeline route, chosen by `useSimpleMode`: `SimpleTimeline.tsx`), in order: the header, Upcoming, My tasks, the tiles with Remove last, `QuickEntryBox size="md" passChips={false}` (writers; the mic tip only when `canDictate`), the full timeline's `FilterChips` (search, Filter, chips; the route builds one for both views), then `TimelineEntryList simple`: rows only, the full timeline's type scale (no `text-2xl`, no zoom), with the full ⋯ menus, edit and pass sheets and delete confirm for writers (locked or not, B-208); no filter links on the kind icon or initials, no period markers; day summaries and the period line (no Change) stay. Blocks above it are cards (`SIMPLE_BLOCK`). Adding, View options and the strips are hidden. Simple variants of these controls may come later; for now they're the full ones.
  - **Upcoming** (`SimpleNow.tsx`): writers get each due pass as a big Given all button with Undo; the next item opens Upcoming's next 5 in place (`SimpleNext`; no dose slots) and See all.
  - **My tasks** (`SimpleTasks.tsx`): my open tasks, at most `SIMPLE_TASKS_CAP`, each with a large tick and Undo; works offline.
  - **Tiles** (writers, `SimpleTiles.tsx`; pure `lib/assistant/simpleTiles.ts`): four links, 2×2 below `sm`, else one row (44 px targets), fixed, learned (most-logged over 30 days) or the group's picked tiles (`care_group.simple_tiles`, via `useSimpleSetup`). Labels use the recipient's pronouns, else their name. Each opens the existing sheet in its **simple variant** (`TimelineOutlet.simple`). While `simple_tiles` is null, owners and admins see a "Choose the tiles" link to Details until a choice is saved.
  - **Remove my last entry** (writers, `removeLast.ts`): your own latest entry from the last 30 minutes (not a medication change), after a confirm.
