# Timeline entries

The timeline area's rules and decisions. An orchestrator folds a landing's lasting decisions for this area in here.

- **Table:** one `timeline_entry` table with a `kind` column.
  - `group_id` and `recipient_id` are on the row (streams filter on them); the composite FK to `care_recipient` is `NO ACTION`, so deleting a recipient with entries fails, while deleting the group cascades.
  - `occurred_at` plus the device's IANA `time_zone`; optional `title`, `body`, and `details` jsonb (a Zod discriminated union per kind).
- **Rules:**
  - **Soft delete** (`deleted_at`), checked against the delete permission. Reads hide deleted rows unless `includeDeleted`.
  - **Keyset pagination** on `(occurred_at, id)`, because offline devices insert older entries.
  - `create` defaults `recipientId` and checks the group first, so a non-member learns nothing. `update` validates the **merged** row.
  - `occurredAt` reads back as Postgres text; normalise it at the API.
- **Kinds.** Place a proposed new thing to log with these first:
  - **Note:** free text about the day. **Its text is its live document**: `body` is always null, the title optional. Written at `/entries/:id`.
  - **Symptom:** something the recipient **feels or reports**: free-text `name` plus `severity` 0-10. **Pain is a symptom.**
  - **Vital:** something **measured, counted or observed**, with a fixed unit or scale (without one, like mood, it's a symptom). Bowel movement, urine output and sleep are vitals.
  - **Dose:** a medication **given, skipped or refused** (`details` has `medicationId` and `status`; no title or body). The medication must be in the entry's group; deleting it leaves its doses.
  - **Medication change:** started, changed, stopped, resumed; **written automatically** (`SYSTEM_WRITTEN_KINDS`), never created or edited by hand, though writers may delete one.
  - **Event:** something that happened. Like a note, **its text is its live document**, written at `/entries/:id`. A title is required: the device saves `"Event"` (`DEFAULT_EVENT_TITLE`) when none is given and the field shows empty with "Add a title" (`isUnnamedEvent`). A future event is an appointment (_Appointments_).
- **Vitals** (`db/schema/timeline/vitals.ts`, the single spec):
  - `details = { readings: [...] }`, at most one per type (types in `VITAL_TYPES`; blood pressure needs diastolic < systolic).
  - **Adding a type is spec-only:** a `VITAL_TYPES` entry, a `VITALS` entry and one `reading(...)` line (new field keys also in `VitalFieldSpec["key"]`).
  - Stored in canonical units (°C, kg, mg/dL, mL); display and entry use the user's units (`fromDisplay`/`toDisplay`).
  - Hard limits reject; a value outside the typical range warns but saves. `isKnownReading()` skips types this build doesn't know.
- **User preferences:** `user.preferences` jsonb (`userPreferencesZSchema`, every field optional, so new settings need no migration): `units`, `timelineView` (`rows | cards`), `daySummary` (`off | highlights | full`, default `highlights`).
- **Timeline screen** (`/app/groups/:groupId/timeline`, `routes/app/groups/timeline/`):
  - Rows (default) or cards, both from `entrySummary()`. Kind icons and colors in `KIND_META`; vitals are pink, **not green** (green reads as "all good"). Day headers use the pure `dayLabel` (`lib/core/format.ts`).
  - **Filters live in the URL** (`?kind=&author=<userId>`, and `?vital=<type>` only under `kind=vital`; changing the kind drops it; Latest vitals ignores it), owned by the pure `lib/timeline/timelineFilters.ts` (`parseFilters` ignores unknown kinds; an author must be UUID-shaped, membership isn't checked). **Every filter control is a `Link` built through it**, with `replace` and `preventScrollReset`. `entriesQuery` and `timelineLinksQuery` share `entryWindow` (`lib/timeline/timeline.sync.ts`), so the "with …" lines cover exactly the listed entries. A filter change starts from one page.
  - **Kind icon** and **author control** (`components/entry/KindIcon.tsx`) are links ("Show only <kind plural>", "Show only <name>'s entries") above the row's open target, for viewers too. An author filter adds a "By <name>" chip clearing only the author (a former member still filters).
  - **Strips** (viewers too; task and medication strips hidden in the simple view): `LatestVitals` (ignores filters; "All vitals" disclosure), `NextStrip` (links to the next entry, with "Show upcoming"), `LiveNowBanner` (who is writing the latest-edited document, with **Join**; rows and cards show a `LiveDot`), the medication strip, and `TaskStrip` (`lib/tasks/taskStrip.ts`: overdue or due-today open tasks, mine first).
  - **Wide layout:** from `lg` the strips and the entries' column stop at set widths (`STRIP_CAP`, `COLUMN_CAP` in `timeline/layout.ts`). **Cards stay one column at every width** (side-by-side breaks the time order).
  - **Adding** (`QuickAddMenu`): a + button "Add to <day label>" beside each day's heading; writers only, not in simple mode. Another day's adds carry `?at=` (`START_AT_PARAM`), set by `dayStartAt`: 12:00 that day in the viewer's zone.
  - **Sheets.** Note and Event open `/entries/new?kind=…`. Symptom and Vitals open the **add-sheet route** `timeline/new/:kind` (`symptom | vital`, `?type=<vitalType>`; `appUrls.newSheet`) in the timeline's `Outlet`: `newSheetGuard` sends another kind or a viewer to the timeline. **Editing** is `timeline/:entryId/edit` (`appUrls.editSheet`): `editSheetGuard` sends a note or event to its page, and a viewer or a missing, deleted or other group's entry to the timeline. `useCloseSheet` goes back when opened from the timeline, else replaces with it, so Back never reopens a saved sheet. **Sheet links use `prefetch="render"`**, or sheets can't open offline after a full load.
  - **Row taps** (`EntryOpenTarget`, one stretched link per row): Notes and events open `/entries/:id` for everyone; symptoms and vitals open their edit sheet for writers and nothing for viewers (`openRule`). ⋯ has Delete and Edit (writers) and **Print** (notes and events, everyone).
  - **The quick-entry box** (writers): `components/quick-entry/`, rules in [assistant](assistant.md).
  - **Day summaries** (`DaySummaryLine` after each `DayHeader`; pure `daySummary.ts`): vitals (a range with an arrow), symptoms (highest severity, "(new)" against the baseline), events, who logged. **No note count and no doses**; a day with no vitals, symptoms or events gets no line. **Highlights** are vitals with an arrow, new or severe (`SEVERE_SYMPTOM`) symptoms, and events. The viewer's `daySummary` setting (the header's `DaySummaryMenu`) picks the collapsed line; the expanded list is always the full breakdown; the simple view follows it. **Any kind, author, vital or search filter hides every summary** (`summariesOff`).
  - **The history ends at now** (`historyWindow`), so future notes and events never sit at the top. `?upcoming=1` adds an **Upcoming block** above Today (up to `UPCOMING_BLOCK_LIMIT`, then "See all in Upcoming"; `aheadQueries.ts`).
  - **Infinite scroll:** `OlderEntries` calls `loadOlder`; the limit grows by `PAGE_SIZE` (`lib/core/paging.ts`; Upcoming's Past tab pages the same way). `useLiveRows` says whether its rows belong to the current query, so the previous window shows until a larger one fully arrives. Smooth to about 300 loaded rows; virtualising is not built.
  - **The route only composes** (`route.tsx`): data from `useTimelineData`, the view from `useTimelineView`, deletes from `useEntryRemoval`. The loader and `meta` share `pageTitle()` on purpose: it keeps them in one chunk.
- **Seeds** (`seeds/timelineEntries.ts`):
  - **Ids follow position**, so a change needs `pnpm datastore:reset`; `timelineEntries.test.ts` validates every entry against the content schema, since seeds bypass the service.
  - A note's or event's seed `body` becomes its document (`seedContentOf`); the entry's `body` stays null.
- **Tests:** the timeline's behaviour is component tests beside the route, on the shared world in `timelineWorld.tsx`; browser checks keep only what needs the server.
- **The simple view** (a view of the timeline route, chosen by `useSimpleMode`: `SimpleTimeline.tsx`): `QuickEntryBox size="lg"` (writers), then `TimelineEntryList simple`: rows only, zoomed, no ⋯ menus, filter links or period markers; day summaries stay. Only notes and events open. Adding, the layout switch, the strips and filter chips are hidden, and `useSimpleModeUrl` replaces a filtered URL with the plain timeline.
  - **The Now block** (`SimpleNow.tsx`): writers get each due pass as a big Given all button with Undo, plus the next appointment; viewers get text only.
  - **My tasks** (`SimpleTasks.tsx`): my open tasks, at most `SIMPLE_TASKS_CAP`, each with a large tick and Undo; works offline.
  - **Tiles** (writers, `SimpleTiles.tsx`; pure `lib/assistant/simpleTiles.ts`): four big links, fixed, learned (most-logged over 30 days) or the group's picked tiles (`care_group.simple_tiles`, via `useSimpleSetup`). Each opens the existing sheet in its **simple variant** (`TimelineOutlet.simple`). While `simple_tiles` is null, owners and admins see only a "Choose the tiles" link to Details; saving any choice, even learned, stores a value and hides it.
  - **Remove my last entry** (writers, `removeLast.ts`): your own latest entry from the last 30 minutes (not a medication change), after a confirm.
