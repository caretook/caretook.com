# The `/app` frame

**The `/app` frame** (`routes/app/layout/route.tsx`, a hot file): fixed regions that slices and PRDs fill through their components, never by restructuring the layout:

- header `group` (left): the Caretook link (mark only below `sm` on group routes), then `GroupMenu` on group routes. The header isn't sticky;
- header `sections` (`hidden sm:flex`): `SectionNav` (icons only below `lg`, `sr-only`; `GroupMenu` truncates); `status`: `SyncIndicator` ("Offline · N waiting"; "N not saved" links to that page); `user`: `UserMenu`;
- under the header: `Announcement` (right after `header`), then `OfflineBanner` (`useSyncState`): a kit `Notice` (warning) overlaid just under the header in a zero-height anchor, so it never shifts the page; grace 10 s when `navigator.onLine` is false, 30 s when online but PowerSync isn't connected, 90 s on dev (container and dev-server restarts);
- the outlet, then `TabBar` (phones, group routes), then `ToastRegion`; `UpdateNotice` toasts a new version · Reload.
- **Starting up:** `root.tsx`'s `HydrateFallback` is `brand/AppLoading`, invisible for 300 ms by CSS (`[data-app-loading]`: it shows before scripts run, so it stays script-free); `brand/LoadingMark` is the look it shares with `ui/PageLoading`, a full-page wait in `/app` (message, optional Go back via history else the groups list, a slow prompt after 10 s).
- **Typeface:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next in `app/fonts/` (`SOURCE.txt`), preloaded by `root.tsx`.
- **The service worker** (web production only; `entry.client.tsx` registers it): `lib/app-shell/serviceWorker*.ts`, bundled to `/sw.js` by `scripts/build/service-worker.mjs`. Install (8 at a time, 4 tries, only 200s) caches the server's `/app` document (no care data), all JS, CSS and `woff2` file in `assets/` and the async SQLite WASM (`SQLITE_WASM`, required). `/app/*` navigations get that shell, other pages the network, else `/offline.html` (B-788); precached assets cache-first, other `/assets/` kept on first success (`routeRequest`). A new worker waits until Reload posts `skip-waiting`. ⚠ The cached shell keeps the CSP of its build until the next worker installs. **Install:** `site.webmanifest` opens `/app` standalone.
- Screens outside a group get the header only. A failed chunk reloads once (`staleChunk.ts`, web).
- **Routes are a nested tree** in `routes.ts` and the area `routes.ts` parts it spreads in: the `app` layout → `home`, `settings`, and the `group` layout (`/app/groups/:groupId`) → an index redirecting to the timeline, `timeline`, `entries/:entryId`, `settings`. **A folder per route:** `routes/app/<area>/<page>/route.tsx`.
- **The group route** (`routes/app/groups/layout/route.tsx`): its `clientMiddleware` loads the group once per navigation, before any child loader. **Client middleware works in both builds.** `lib/app-shell/group.tsx`: `setGroup` records the group or `notOnDevice` and **never throws** (native's first load would crash). Every loader and action under it calls `getGroup(context)` first. Components read `useGroup()`, live (when the row goes, to `appUrls.groupList()` with a lost-access notice). Screens never query the group, role or membership themselves.
- **`appUrls`** (`lib/app-shell/appUrls.ts`, composed from `lib/<area>/urls.ts`) builds every `/app/…` path, carrying search params. **Nothing else writes an `/app/…` string**.
- **`UserMenu`:** avatar, Settings, **Theme** (System, Light, Dark), Sign out (POST `/sign-out`).
- **Theme** (`lib/app-shell/theme.ts`): per device in `caretook:theme`, never sent; kept at sign-out. Light and Dark set `data-theme` on `<html>`; System removes it. `THEME_SCRIPT` (first in `root.tsx`'s `<head>`) applies it before paint.
- **`GroupMenu`:** every local group (live, offline), the current one checked; one opens its timeline. The trigger shows the care period's icon. Then "New care group" (`NEW_GROUP_LINK_STATE`), a separator, the profile, Care periods and Group settings.
- **Last group** (`lib/app-shell/lastGroup.ts`): a group route you belong to is saved per user; sign-out forgets it. `/app`'s loader redirects by the pure `resolveHomeGroup`: the remembered group if still yours, else the only group, else the list.
- **Group sections** (`lib/app-shell/groupSections.ts`): `GROUP_SECTIONS`, an ordered `{ id, label, icon, path(groupId), match(subpath) }` list (Timeline's `match` also claims `/entries/*`). Four: Timeline, Providers, Medications (opens Schedule), Planner (`match`: `/planner…`, `/todos…`). **A new section is one appended entry and its route.** `SectionNav` and `TabBar` render it (`prefetch="render"`, `aria-current` from the pending navigation). Hidden on `/entries/*`; else an in-flow spacer clears it; bottom-fixed things offset by `--tab-bar-height`. **`TabBar`** (phones; `[data-tab-bar]`): 3rem (`--tab-bar-bar`), on `--tab-bar-glass` (light `base-100` 85%, dark `base-300` 70%, blurred; solid without `backdrop-filter`); icon-only links; the current one on a `primary` pill (`data-tab-bar-indicator`) that slides (not under reduced motion).
- **Section accents** (`lib/app-shell/sectionAccents.ts`): Timeline indigo, Medications teal, Planner orange, Providers lime, Care periods plum (`MapPin`); `sectionAccent(id, { easy })` gives nothing when easy.
- **Group settings** (`/app/groups/:groupId/settings`, `routes/app/groups/settings/`; not a section): the same `SettingsFrame` as account settings, with `groupSettingsPages(groupId)` (`layout/settingsPages.ts`) and `appUrls.groupSettings`. Subpages: **Details** (the group card, the recipient's **time zone**), **Members**, **Assistants** ([connectors](connectors.md)) and **Calendar & reminders** (`appUrls.calendarSettings`, [calendars](calendars.md); it holds the owners' details switch). **Writes are online only**: each subpage's `clientAction` calls oRPC. Offline or without the role, a card is read-only and says "Needs a connection to edit".
  - **Members:** live from local data, sorted by role then name. Online, owners and admins get a role select and Remove; everyone gets Leave on their own row. Only owners are offered Owner or controls on an owner's row.
  - **Invites** (beside `members/route.tsx`): owners and admins get **Invite by email** (with the attestation checkbox) and a **Pending** list; offline it's disabled. Invite links and a **Requests** list (Approve, Decline) sit beside them. The requester's `/app` home shows the request's state (`home/PendingRequests.tsx`), hidden once the group is on the device; a waiting request can be **Withdrawn** (online only).
- **New care group** (`/app/groups/new`, `appUrls.newGroup()`; under the app layout, not the group route): a `Sheet` with `NewGroupFields`, shared with `/join/:code` (`components/groups/`). Online only; after `careGroup.create` it waits up to 10 s (`waitForMembership`) and opens the new timeline, else `/app`.
- **Setup card** (`timeline/GettingStartedCard.tsx`, `lib/app-shell/gettingStarted.ts`), both timelines: 14 days from joining; writers get steps from synced data, others a `WelcomeCard`; `preferences.setupDismissed` only grows. Then a lone manager gets `InviteSomeoneCard`.
- **`/join/:code`** (shared, client-only): an account form, then "Who do you care for?"; one call creates everything, the email's `callbackURL` is `/app`, and the page goes to `/verify-email` for every address. Signed in → points to New care group.
- **`/admin/*`** (web only, server loaders through `.server/admin.ts`): staff only, by capability (see [admin](admin.md)); anyone else gets a 404 (group owners too), signed-out visitors go to sign-in. Admin screens show no care data.
  - **`/invite/:token`** (`routes/account/invite/`, shared, client-only): preview → sign up with the email locked (or sign in) → consent → Join, or Decline; a wrong-account view offers Sign out and comes back; dead links don't name the group. After Join it waits for the membership to sync, then opens the group.
  - **Leaving waits** up to 10 s for the membership to sync away (`waitForMembershipGone`).
- **Settings** (`/app/settings`, `routes/app/settings/`): subpages from the `ACCOUNT_SETTINGS` registry, rendered by `SettingsFrame`: one `nav[data-settings-nav]` with `aria-current` (`settingsCurrent`); a tapped page spins until it renders. On phones the index is the list; from `md` it's a side nav and the index redirects to the first subpage. Subpages (`appUrls.settings`): Units, **Timeline layout** (`saveSettings` on change; `loadSettings` shows a pending change at once), Account (`AvatarCard` first, a live preview), Contact details, **Changes not saved** (`not-saved`: the device's refused changes, one line per row, Dismiss, Dismiss all; local, offline), and **Devices** (last): Better Auth's session list and revokes, then Connected assistants, online only.
- **Easy mode** (the view is in [timeline](timeline.md)): `resolveEasyMode({ role, ownSetting, deviceToggle })` (`lib/assistant/easyMode.ts`) decides it: an easy role (Easy helper, Easy viewer) is always easy and has no toggle; anyone else gets this device's toggle (`easyModeToggle.ts`, per user and group in localStorage; cleared by a change of their setting and `wipeDevice`), then their own setting (`user.preferences.easyMode`, `easy | full`, on the Timeline layout page; a stored `group` reads full). `EasyModeToggle` sits in the timeline's header. The frame (`layout/useEasyFrame.ts`) and `UserMenu easy` key on `easyRole`: an easy role gets the reduced header (mark, search magnifier, sync status) on every page of its group, and the menu adds "Your care groups" when they have several (`appUrls.groupList()`, `/app?groups=1`: no remembered-group redirect); anyone else gets it only on the timeline in easy mode.
- **The easy-role allow-list** (`lib/assistant/easyModeLock.ts`: `easyRoleAllows`, `easyRoleRedirect`, pure): an easy role may stay on the timeline and its sheets, `entries/*`, `/planner` (not Past or Todos), a todo's page `todos/<id>` and `/search`; every other group route redirects to the timeline. It's enforced once, in the group layout route (middleware, plus `EasyRoleRedirect` for a role that syncs in while elsewhere), never per route; account settings and `/app` stay reachable.
