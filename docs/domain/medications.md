# Medications

The medications area's rules and decisions. An orchestrator folds a landing's lasting decisions for this area in here.

PRD: [`plans/medications-prd.md`](../../plans/medications-prd.md).

Code: schema `packages/data/src/db/schema/medications/` (and `profiles/recipientAllergy.ts`); logic `apps/web/app/lib/medications/`; screens `routes/app/groups/medications/`, `medication/`; `components/medications/`.

- **Table** (`medication`): `group_id`, `recipient_id` (composite FK to `care_recipient`), `schedule` jsonb, `type` (`prescription | otc | supplement | other`; nullable, checked, no backfill; required where asked via `medicationFormContentZSchema` and `requireType`; not tracked by `medicationChange`), `status` `current | completed | discontinued`, `stop_reason` (`sideEffects | ineffective | prescriberStopped | other`), refills, `prescriber_id` and `pharmacy_id` (composite FKs to `provider`, **`NO ACTION`**: Drizzle can't express a column-list `SET NULL`; providers are soft-deleted), `deleted_at`. Members read, writers write, soft delete.
- **Schedule:** `{ type: "scheduled", slots: [{ passId, amount? } | { time, amount? }] }` (1-8 slots, no repeats) or `{ type: "asNeeded", maxPerDay? }` (1-24), **in the recipient's time zone**. **Every read goes through `normalizeSchedule`** (also accepts the old `{ times }`); writes use `slots` only. Missed doses are never written.
- **Passes** (`medication_pass`): a group's named rounds (`name`, `time` `HH:MM` recipient-zone, `sort_order`). Live names are unique per group (case-insensitive), at most 8. **Write through `medicationPassService`:** a retime writes one `medicationChange` entry (`passMoved`, id `passMovedId(passId, changedAt)`); a delete turns its slots into `{ time, amount }` in every live medication's schedule in the same transaction, no entries. The device (`medicationPasses.sync.ts`) does both in one local transaction; the service repeats them idempotently. **Default passes** (Morning 08:00, Evening 20:00): `medicationPassService.addDefaults`, called by every group-create path.
- **Allergies** (`recipient_allergy`; `severity` `mild | moderate | severe | unknown`; soft deletes), through `recipientAllergyService`. `care_recipient.no_known_allergies_at` records "No known allergies" and is cleared when an allergy is added; recording it with allergies on file is refused (`hasAllergies`). Wording and matching in `allergies.ts`, device `allergies.sync.ts`. `AllergiesRegion` shows them; `PrintAllergies` tops both printed sheets, `PrintConditions` under it (active conditions via `conditionText`, or "No known conditions"; left out when none or all past); `NameField` warns on a name match.
- **What it's for** (`medication_condition`, `medicationConditionService`; conditions are in [profiles](profiles.md)): links from a medication to the recipient's conditions, beside the free-text reason. The form's For field (`ConditionsField`: active conditions, plus a resolved one already linked) posts `conditionIds`. A save checks them, writes the medication, then the links (`checkMedicationConditions`, `setMedicationConditions`): a refusal leaves nothing. The page's For row links each to its sheet.
- **Status rules:** completed and discontinued need `stopped_on`; discontinued needs `stop_reason`; current clears the stop fields; completed clears the reason. The prescriber must be a live clinician or practice in the group, the pharmacy a live pharmacy, checked only when set or changed. Status actions go through `changeMedicationStatus` or `removeMedication`, which write the change entry.
- **Change entries** (pure `medicationChange(before, after)`): create, a status change and an edit to a tracked field (name, strength, form, dose, directions, schedule) write one entry **in one transaction**; refill, reason, provider and type edits write none. Its id is `medicationChangeId(medicationId, changedAt)` (UUID v5), `changedAt` its `occurred_at`. The device writes the medication, then the entry; on upload the service writes the same entry from the medication op (`updated_at` as `changedAt`), so its own PUT is an idempotent upsert.
- **Documents:** `note_document`'s `medication` subject (`medication_id`); `noteDocumentService.create` takes a live one.
- **Sync:** in the `care_groups` stream and the publication (its migration's hand-written `ALTER PUBLICATION` stays when regenerating); upload is `medicationOp` ([sync](sync.md)). Device `medications.sync.ts`: queries, local writes (medication and change entry in one transaction); wording is pure `medicationDisplay.ts`.
- **Screens** (`GROUP_SECTIONS`, claiming `/medications/*`):
  - **The medication page** (`medications/:medicationId`): details (a Type row; labels from `medicationTypeDisplay.ts`), prescriber and pharmacy as provider links ("(removed)" if deleted), its live notes (made on the first change; viewers read only), open linked tasks (`LinkedTasks`, [tasks](tasks.md)), and the dose and change history. Missing, deleted or other-group ones 404.
  - **Add and edit** are sheet routes (`new`, `:medicationId/edit`): a new provider is held as a name until Save, then `newProviders` inserts it first in the same transaction. The form's Type `ChoiceGroup` (Prescription on new) and "Prescription details" (`<details>`). `medicationSheets.ts` guards send viewers and missing, deleted or other-group medications to the list; closing returns via `MEDICATION_SHEET_STATE`.
  - The provider page has a "Medications" region (`ProviderMedications`); pure `medicationList.ts` filters the list (`?q=`, `?status=`, `?type=`: `setType`/`clearType`, `TypeFilter` chips); rows show a type badge.
- **Printable sheets** (local data, offline too): the medication list (`print/`, pure `printSheet(...)`: `currentGroups` by type under `medicationTypeGroupHeading`, each by name; stopped, ungrouped, within 90 days with how and why) and the weekly bedside sheet (`print/week?start=YYYY-MM-DD`, `weeklySheet.ts`: seven days, default this Monday; a group per pass; landscape via `@page weekly`, `[page:weekly]`), both from the list's Print `Menu`. Print CSS (`app.css`) forces black on white; the `/app` header and tab bar are `print:hidden`.
- **What's due** (pure `dueDoses.ts` in `@caretook/data/schema` with `duePasses`, `givenAllWrites`, `zonedDay`; the one source for Today, its card, the Dose sheet and the connector's `todays_doses`, `give_pass`): `dueDoses({ medications, doses, now, timeZone, overdueAfterMinutes = 60, windowMinutes = 120 })` expands yesterday's and today's slots in the recipient's zone (`zonedDay`: a skipped time moves forward, a repeated one is the first). A dose with `scheduledFor` answers the nearest slot with that time only; without one, the nearest open slot within the window. States: upcoming, due, overdue, given, skipped, refused; only yesterday's open slots are missed. Only current, live medications, none before added or started. `doseWarnings`: `alreadyGiven`, `overMax`. `useDueDoses` (`dueDoses.sync.ts`) re-evaluates every 30 s, not `ready` until its queries answer.
- **By pass:** a slot is keyed by its pass when it has one, so a retimed pass keeps its answered doses; a dose answers by `passId` first, then `scheduledFor`. Doses carry optional `passId` and `passLogId` (a round's id). **`duePasses`** rolls slots into passes (read by Today, the strip, the Dose sheet, Upcoming); `doseWarnings` stays per medication. **Given all** (`givenAllWrites`: open slots only, one round, reusing a started one) writes in one local transaction (`logDoses`); **Undo** removes exactly those doses (`removeDoses`) via one shared offer (`passUndo.ts`, 10 s) and `usePassLog`.
- **Today** (top of the list; `TodaySection`): each pass with a state badge; writers get Given all, one-tap **Given** (now, with `scheduledFor` and the dose), Skipped and Refused; as-needed rows ask before passing the maximum.
- **The Today card's medications row** (`TodayMedicationsRow`): pure `medicationStrip.ts` picks the pass (overdue, else due, else next; tomorrow's first if none today). Writers get a Dose sheet link (`?pass=`) and Given all while due. The Dose sheet lists due passes, then due slots, warning before saving ("Log anyway").
- **Pass logs on the timeline:** doses sharing a `passLogId` fold into one item per day at the earliest dose's time (`groupPassLogs` in `passLogs.ts`; `foldPassLogs` in `timeline/dayItems.ts` runs after the care period markers, paging never splitting one (`wholeLogsPage`)). Writers edit every dose together on the **pass sheet** (`timeline/pass/:passLogId/edit`, `updateDoses`).
- **On the timeline:** both kinds are in `TIMELINE_KINDS` and the chips. Medications come from one live query over the window (`timelineMedicationsQuery`), never one per row. A dose opens its edit sheet for writers; a medication change opens nothing, its ⋯ Delete only (`openRule`).
- **The Dose sheet** (`timeline/new/dose`; `appUrls.newDose`): the amount follows the medication's dose until typed; a hidden `scheduledFor` comes from `?slot=HH:MM`. `newDoseGuard` sends viewers to the timeline and drops an unknown medication or bad slot. Its last option **"+ New medication"** (`new`) adds: name (`NameField`, in `components/medications`), dose, type, as needed (no maximum). Pure `quickAddRows` and `logDoseOfNewMedication` write the medication, its "started" entry and the dose in one transaction; no dose warnings. Then the timeline shows `AddedMedicationToast` "Added <name> · Add details" (`addedNotice.ts`, ~10 s, gone on navigation). With no medications the sheet opens on the quick add.
- **Doses over a range:** `doseSlotsBetween(...)` (DST-safe; a stopped medication counts to `stoppedOn`, deleted ones never) and `missedDoseCounts(slots)`, used by visit prep. `logDoses` is `doseRows` (check, plan) plus the write, so quick entry's runner plans in its own transaction.
- **Seeds** (`seeds/medications.ts`, `medicationPasses.ts`): both groups have default passes, typed medications and America/Los_Angeles recipients; doses fill the timeline's first page.
- **Tests:** component tests build on `medicationWorld.tsx` (`fixClock`/`clockAt`); `syncMedications.int.test.ts` covers an offline add, edit and discontinue in one batch.
