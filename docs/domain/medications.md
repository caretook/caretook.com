# Medications

The medications area's rules and decisions, moved out of AGENTS.md (whose core rules still apply). An orchestrator folds a landing's lasting decisions for this area in here.

PRD: [`plans/medications-prd.md`](../../plans/medications-prd.md).

- **Table** (`db/schema/medication.ts`): `medication` with `group_id`, `recipient_id` (composite FK to `care_recipient`), `name`, `strength`, `form` (nullable: `tablet | capsule | liquid | injection | patch | inhaler | drops | cream | other`), `dose`, `directions`, `reason`, `schedule` jsonb, `status` (`current | completed | discontinued`), `started_on`, `stopped_on`, `stop_reason` (`sideEffects | ineffective | prescriberStopped | other`), `stop_note`, `prescriber_id` and `pharmacy_id` (composite FKs to `provider`, **`NO ACTION`**: Drizzle 0.45 can't express a column-list `SET NULL`, and providers are only soft-deleted), refill fields (`last_filled_on`, `quantity`, `refills_left`, `rx_number`), `deleted_at`. Unique `(group_id, id)`. Permissions: any member reads, writers write, delete is soft.
- **Schedule:** `{ type: "scheduled", slots: [{ passId, amount? } | { time, amount? }] }` (1–8 slots, no repeated pass or time) or `{ type: "asNeeded", maxPerDay? }` (1–24), **in the recipient's time zone**. **Every read goes through `normalizeSchedule`** (`db/schema/medication.ts`), which also accepts the old `{ type: "scheduled", times }` as time slots (older builds may upload it); writes use `slots` only. Missed doses are worked out, never written.
- **Passes** (`medication_pass`, `db/schema/medicationPass.ts`; medications-2): a group's named rounds (`name`, `time` `HH:MM` in the recipient's zone, `sort_order`, soft `deleted_at`). Live names are unique per group, case-insensitive (a partial unique index on `lower(name)`), at most 8 live (`MEDICATION_PASS_LIMITS`). Pure rules there: `sortPasses`, `passNameTaken`, `canAddPass`, `reorderPasses`, `withoutPass`, `slotTimes`, `medicationsInPass`. **Write through `medicationPassService`:** a retime writes one `medicationChange` entry with the `passMoved` variant (pass, name, before and after, the medication ids; id `passMovedId(passId, changedAt)`, UUID v5), and a delete turns its slots into `{ time, amount }` in every live medication's schedule in the same transaction, with no change entries for those. The device (`lib/sync/medicationPasses.ts`: `createPass`, `updatePass`, `movePass`, `removePass`) does both in one local transaction; the service repeats them idempotently on upload. **Default passes** (`DEFAULT_PASSES`: Morning 08:00, Evening 20:00) come from `medicationPassService.addDefaults`, called by every group-create path (`careGroupService.create`, the access-code redemption, the staff create); no backfill. The **Passes screen** (`medications/passes`, `appUrls.medicationPasses`, linked from the list header): passes in order with their medications; writers add, rename, retime, reorder and delete (a confirm saying the medications keep the time), offline; viewers read.
- **Allergies** (`recipient_allergy`, `db/schema/recipientAllergy.ts`; medications-2): `substance`, `reaction`, `severity` (`mild | moderate | severe | unknown`), soft deletes, through `recipientAllergyService`. `care_recipient.no_known_allergies_at` records "No known allergies" and is cleared when an allergy is added; recording it with allergies on file is refused (`hasAllergies`). Pure wording and matching in `lib/allergies.ts` (`allergyState`, `allergySummary`, `allergyMatches`, `allergyWarning`); device in `lib/sync/allergies.ts`. Shown by `components/medications`' `AllergiesRegion` on the list and a medication's page, and `PrintAllergies` at the top of both printed sheets. Edited in the **allergies sheet** (`medications/allergies`, writers only, `appUrls.medicationAllergies`; closing returns where it was opened through link state). `NameField` warns when a medication's name matches an allergy.
- **Status rules:** completed and discontinued need `stopped_on`; discontinued needs `stop_reason`; current clears the stop fields; completed clears the reason. The prescriber must be a live clinician or practice in the group and the pharmacy a live pharmacy, checked only when set or changed.
- **Change entries** (`medicationChange(before, after)`, pure): create, a status change and an edit to a tracked field (name, strength, form, dose, directions, schedule) write one `medicationChange` entry **in the same transaction**; refill, reason and provider edits write none. Its id is `medicationChangeId(medicationId, changedAt)` (UUID v5), and `changedAt` is also its `occurred_at`. The device writes the medication, then the entry; on upload the service writes the same entry from the medication op (the upload passes the device's `updated_at` as `changedAt`), so the entry's own PUT is an idempotent upsert that returns the service's copy.
- **Documents:** `note_document`'s `medication` subject with `medication_id` (a cascading composite FK, on the exactly-one-owner check). `noteDocumentService.create` accepts a medication that isn't deleted.
- **Sync:** `medication` in the `care_groups` stream and the publication (the hand-written `ALTER PUBLICATION` at the end of its migration: keep it when regenerating). The upload switch's `medicationOp`: PUT upsert, PATCH update or soft remove, DELETE soft remove; a changed group or recipient is `immutable`.
- **Device** (`lib/sync/medications.ts`): queries (the list with search and status, one medication, its doses and changes, the group's doses between two moments, name history, the recipient), `dayInZone` and `dayBounds` (the recipient's day, DST-safe), and local writes (`createMedication`, `updateMedication`, `changeMedicationStatus`, `removeMedication`, each with its change entry in one transaction; `logDose`, `updateDose`, `removeMedicationEntry`). `createMedicationDocument` writes a medication's notes document (`subject: "medication"`), and `providerMedicationsQuery` lists the current medications naming a provider.
- **Wording** (`lib/medicationDisplay.ts`, pure, tested; the one home for it): labels for forms, statuses and stop reasons, `scheduleText` ("8:00 and 20:00", "As needed, up to 4 a day"), `medicationTitle`, `doseText` ("Given 1 tablet"), `changeText` ("Changed strength 5 mg → 10 mg", "Discontinued: side effects", "Resumed; changed dose …"), `stoppedText`, `scheduleZoneNote` ("Times are Los Angeles time" when the device is elsewhere). `lib/medicationEntries.ts` re-exports it for the timeline and adds `medicationLabel`, `withMedication` and `entryMedication`.
- **Screens** (children of the group route; a Medications entry in `GROUP_SECTIONS` after Providers, lucide `Pill`, claiming `/medications/*`; built through `appUrls.medications`, `medication`, `medicationsPrint`, `newDose`):
  - **The list** (`routes/app/groups/medications/`): live, current first by name, then "Completed and discontinued"; search (`?q=`, every word in the name or reason) and a status chip (`?status=`; `all` or unknown is none), through the pure `lib/medicationList.ts`. A **Print** link in the header, and "Add medication" for writers.
  - **The medication page** (`routes/app/groups/medication/`, `medications/:medicationId`): header regions (title, details, editors, actions: Edit and the status ⋯ for writers), details with the schedule's zone note, prescriber and pharmacy as provider links with `ContactActions` (greyed "(removed)" when deleted), refills, the live notes (created on the first real change; read-only for viewers), and the history of doses and changes, newest first. Missing, deleted or other-group medications are the 404.
  - **The printable sheet** (`medications/print`, `routes/app/groups/medications/print/`): the pure `printSheet({ recipient, groupName, medications, providers, now })` (current by name; stopped within 90 days, newest first, with how and why; prescribers and pharmacies once each, with their first phone; the day printed in the recipient's zone). Local data only, so it works offline. **Print** calls `window.print()`. Print CSS (`@media print` in `app.css`) forces black on white in every theme, keeps table rows whole and repeats headers; the `/app` header and the tab bar carry `print:hidden`. Reuse both for B-51.
  - **The provider page** gets a "Medications" region (`ProviderMedications`): the current medications that name it ("Prescribes" or "Fills"), hidden when there are none.
- **Adding and editing** (sheet routes `medications/new` and `medications/:medicationId/edit`, children of the list; `appUrls.newMedication`, `editMedication`): `MedicationForm` with `NameField` (suggestions from `suggestMedicationNames` in `lib/medicationNames.ts`: prefix first, then a later word, case and accents ignored, at most 5; a chip fills the name only), `ScheduleFields` (set times, up to 8, or as needed with an optional most-in-a-day), and `ProviderPickerField` (`ComboBox` over clinicians and practices, or pharmacies, with "Add "<typed>" as a new prescriber/pharmacy"; a new provider is held as a name until Save, then `createMedication`/`updateMedication`'s `newProviders` inserts it first, in the medication's transaction). Pure and tested: `medicationFormData.ts`, `medicationProviders.ts`. The guards (`lib/medicationSheets.ts`) send viewers and missing, deleted or other-group medications to the list. Closing goes back when opened from the list or the page (`MEDICATION_SHEET_STATE`, `useCloseMedicationSheet`), so Edit from the page returns to it; a deep link closes to the list.
- **Linked tasks** (tasks PRD): a medication's page lists its open linked tasks after refills (`LinkedTasks` from `components/tasks`), with "Add task" prefilled with `medicationId`; see [tasks](tasks.md).
- **Status actions** (`components/medications/MedicationStatusActions`: a ⋯ `Menu` on the page, a "Status" row in the edit sheet; posts parsed by `lib/medicationStatusForm.ts`): Mark completed (a confirm with the stop day), Discontinue (a confirm with a required reason, a note and the day), Restart (at once), Delete (soft, then the list). Each goes through `changeMedicationStatus` or `removeMedication`, so the change entry is written with it.
- **What's due** (`lib/dueDoses.ts`, pure, tested; the one source for Today, the strip and the Dose sheet): `dueDoses({ medications, doses, now, timeZone, overdueAfterMinutes = 60, windowMinutes = 120 })` expands yesterday's and today's slots in the recipient's zone (`lib/zonedDay.ts`: a skipped time moves forward by the gap, a repeated one is the first). A dose with `scheduledFor` answers the nearest slot with that time and never moves to another; without one (or with a time no longer scheduled) it answers the nearest open slot within the window. States: upcoming, due, overdue, given, skipped, refused; only yesterday's open slots are missed. Only current, live medications, and none before they were added or started. As needed: today's given count, the maximum, what's left. `doseWarnings` gives `alreadyGiven` and `overMax` (doses marked given only). Missed doses are never written. On the device, `useDueDoses` (`lib/sync/dueDoses.ts`) re-evaluates every 30 s and stays not-`ready` until its queries answer, so nothing flashes as due; `loadDueDoses` is for loaders.
- **By pass** (medications-2): `dueDoses` keys a slot by its pass when it has one, so a retimed pass keeps its answered doses; a dose answers a pass slot by `passId` first, then `scheduledFor`. Doses carry optional `passId` and `passLogId` (one round's shared id; `scheduledFor` stays the slot's time). **`duePasses`** (`lib/duePasses.ts`) rolls slots up into passes (a time of its own is a pass of one) with a state, what's open, who logged what and the round; Today, the strip, the Dose sheet and Upcoming all read it, while `doseWarnings` stays per medication. **Given all** (`givenAllWrites`: open slots only, one round, reusing a round already started) writes in one local transaction (`logDoses`), and **Undo** removes exactly those doses (`removeDoses`): one shared offer (`lib/passUndo.ts`, 10 s) and one polite `PassUndoStatus` line, through `usePassLog` (`components/medications`). Pass cards are `PassCard` with `DoseSlotRow`s.
- **Today** (top of the medications list; `TodaySection` with `PassCard`s, `AsNeededRow`, `useLogDose`): each pass with its medications and a state badge, Given all for writers; writers get one-tap **Given** (logged now with `scheduledFor` and the medication's dose) and a ⋯ with Skipped and Refused (a `ConfirmDialog` with an optional note); as-needed rows say "2 of 4 today" with Log (past the maximum it asks first); yesterday's missed doses on one line; the zone note when the device is elsewhere. Hidden when nothing is scheduled; viewers get no controls.
- **The medication strip** (`MedicationStrip` and `useMedicationStrip` in the timeline folder, after `LiveNowBanner`; replaced Due now): one line on `STRIP_CLASS`, always shown while the group has scheduled medications. The pure `lib/medicationStrip.ts` picks the pass (earliest overdue, else due, else the next upcoming, else tomorrow's first) and words it ("Overdue: Morning · 8:00 · 2 meds · +1 more"; 24-hour times like Today); due is amber, overdue red. Writers get the pass name as a link to the Dose sheet on that pass (`?pass=`) and Given all while it's due; viewers get text. The Dose sheet lists due passes first, expanded, with Given all and Undo, then due slots ("Metformin 500 mg · 20:00 dose, overdue"; a slot choice posts as `medicationId="<id>@HH:MM"`), and shows a warning before saving, with "Log anyway". Upcoming shows one row per open pass ("Morning · 3 medications").
- **Pass logs on the timeline** (medications-2): within a day, doses sharing a `passLogId` fold into one item at the earliest dose's time (`groupPassLogs` in `lib/passLogs.ts`; a log folds when it names a pass or has 2+ doses), rendered by `PassLogItem` ("Morning: 3 given, 1 refused", `passLogText`) with a "Show medications" toggle. `foldPassLogs` (`timeline/dayItems.ts`) folds them after `withMarkers` placed the care period markers. Paging never splits a log (`wholeLogsPage`). Writers open the **pass sheet** (`timeline/pass/:passLogId/edit`, `appUrls.passSheet`; `lib/sync/passLogs.ts`): every dose's status, amount, time and note saved together (`updateDoses`), Add a medication (same round), Delete this log; viewers and missing or fully deleted logs go to the timeline.
- **The weekly bedside sheet** (`medications/print/week?start=YYYY-MM-DD`, `appUrls.medicationsPrintWeek`; `print/week/weeklySheet.ts`, pure): seven days in the recipient's zone from the start (default this week's Monday), one group per pass in pass order then times of their own, a tick box and initials per day, two blank rows per as-needed medication, the allergies on top; landscape (`@page weekly`, opted in with `[page:weekly]`). The list's **Print** is a `Menu` (`PrintMenu`) with Medication list and Weekly chart, both prefetched so they open offline.
- **On the timeline:** both kinds are in `TIMELINE_KINDS` and the kind chips; `KIND_META` gives dose `Pill` (solid primary) and change `PillBottle` (primary tint), never green or pink. Rows and cards render "<Medication> <strength>" (a link to its page, above the open target; greyed "(removed)" when deleted) with `doseText` or `changeText` and a note line. The medications come from one live query over the timeline's window (`timelineMedicationsQuery` in `lib/sync/medicationTimeline.ts`, called from `useTimelineData`), never one per row; a row can say "Medication" for a moment after a filter change. A dose opens its edit sheet for writers (Edit, Delete); a medication change opens nothing and its ⋯ is Delete only (`openRule`, `entryActionItems`, `isSystemWrittenKind`). `SHOWN_KINDS_SQL` stays as the guard for kinds a build doesn't show.
- **The Dose sheet** (`timeline/new/dose`, route folder `timeline/new-dose/`; `appUrls.newDose(groupId, { medicationId, slot, search })`): medication and status through `ChoiceGroup`, an amount that follows the medication's dose until typed, a time (`DateTimeField`, now by default), a note, and a hidden `scheduledFor` from `?slot=HH:MM`. `newDoseGuard` sends viewers to the timeline and drops an unknown medication or a bad slot. Editing a dose is the same edit sheet with the medication fixed (`updateDose`); a change's edit URL goes to the timeline. The choices are `doseChoicesQuery` (current medications, most recently given first).
- **Seeds** (`seeds/medications.ts` and `seeds/medicationPasses.ts`, own ids; both groups have the default passes and the seeded medications sit on them): Grandma Pat's four current medications (Lisinopril, Metformin twice daily, Acetaminophen as needed, Latanoprost with refills), a completed Amoxicillin and a discontinued Oxybutynin (side effects), about a week of doses (some skipped, one refused; today's evening slots left open), their change entries; Tamsulosin for Grandpa Lou. Both recipients are in America/Los_Angeles.
- **Browser check:** `medications.mjs` (in the suite; its proxy is 9435, since 9434 plus a slot offset can land on Ollama's 11434; each step group opens its own page by URL): the device copy, the tab and header link, the list with search and status, adding (with an inline prescriber and name suggestions), editing, the status actions, offline form saves, viewers, the page, two-browser notes, offline, the 404s, the timeline's doses and changes, the Dose sheet (log, edit, earlier day, delete, offline, a second device, viewer), the print sheet (print media at 420 and 1280, offline), the provider page's region, Today, Given all and Undo, the medication strip, pass logs and the pass sheet, the Passes screen, allergies, the weekly sheet (its own tagged passes and medications with slots a few minutes back in Los Angeles, so it never depends on the clock; one run in America/New_York; each in its own tab, after the timeline steps, because the extra loads hit Better Auth's per-page session limit and new medications' change entries push seeded ones off the first page). Its runs' medications are tagged and soft-deleted at the end.
- **Seeded doses fill the timeline's first page**: a check that looks for an entry a few days back should filter by kind (`live-notes.mjs` uses `?kind=note`).
- **Doses over a range** (assistant slice 7, `lib/dueDoses.ts`): `dueDoses`'s expand, answer and settle steps are shared helpers, and `doseSlotsBetween({ medications, passes, doses, from, to, timeZone })` is its range version (DST-safe; a stopped medication counts to its `stoppedOn`, deleted ones not at all; the current schedule is read for the whole range, so an edit inside it is read as if it always was). `missedDoseCounts(slots)` counts missed and refused per medication; visit prep uses both. `logDoses` is `doseRows` (check and plan) plus the write, so quick entry's runner plans a pass's rows inside its own transaction. The Undo offer can carry `entryIds` and a `place` (`undoFor(groupId, { now, place })`, `PassUndoStatus place`); offers without a place behave as before.
