# Medications

PRD: [`plans/medications-prd.md`](../../plans/medications-prd.md).

Code: schema `packages/data/src/db/schema/medications/` (and `profiles/recipientAllergy.ts`); logic `apps/web/app/lib/medications/`; screens `routes/app/groups/medications/`, `medication/`; `components/medications/`.

- **Table** (`medication`): `group_id`, `recipient_id` (composite FK to `care_recipient`), `schedule` jsonb, `type` (`prescription | otc | supplement | other`; nullable, checked; required via `medicationFormContentZSchema`/`requireType`; untracked by `medicationChange`), `status` `current | completed | discontinued`, `stop_reason` (`sideEffects | ineffective | prescriberStopped | other`), refills, `prescriber_id` and `pharmacy_id` (composite FKs to `provider`, **`NO ACTION`**: Drizzle can't express a column-list `SET NULL`; providers are soft-deleted), `deleted_at`. Members read, writers write, soft delete.
- **Schedule:** `{ type: "scheduled", slots: [{ passId, amount? } | { time, amount? }] }` (1-8 slots, no repeats) or `{ type: "asNeeded", maxPerDay? }` (1-24), **in the recipient's time zone**. **Every read goes through `normalizeSchedule`** (also accepts the old `{ times }`); writes use `slots` only. Missed doses are never written.
- **Passes** (`medication_pass`): a group's named rounds (`name`, `time` `HH:MM` recipient-zone, `sort_order`). Live names unique per group (any case), at most 8. **Write through `medicationPassService`:** a retime writes one `medicationChange` entry (`passMoved`, id `passMovedId(passId, changedAt)`); a delete turns its slots into `{ time, amount }` in every live medication's schedule in the same transaction, no entries. The device (`medicationPasses.sync.ts`) does both locally; the service repeats them idempotently. **Default passes** (Morning 08:00, Evening 20:00): `medicationPassService.addDefaults`, called by every group-create path.
- **Allergies** (`recipient_allergy`; `severity` `mild | moderate | severe | unknown`; soft deletes), through `recipientAllergyService`. `care_recipient.no_known_allergies_at` records "No known allergies", cleared by an added allergy and refused with allergies on file (`hasAllergies`). Wording and matching in `allergies.ts`, device `allergies.sync.ts`. `AllergiesRegion` shows them; `PrintAllergies` tops both printed sheets, then `PrintConditions` (active ones via `conditionText`; none or all past: left out); `NameField` warns on a name match.
- **What it's for** (`medication_condition`, `medicationConditionService`; [profiles](profiles.md)): links to the recipient's conditions, beside the free-text reason. The form's For field (`ConditionsField`: active conditions, plus a resolved one already linked) posts `conditionIds`. A save checks them, then writes the medication and links (`checkMedicationConditions`, `setMedicationConditions`); a refusal leaves nothing. The page's For row links each to its sheet.
- **Status rules:** completed and discontinued need `stopped_on`, discontinued a `stop_reason`; current clears both, completed the reason. The prescriber must be a live clinician or practice, the pharmacy a live pharmacy (checked when set or changed). Status actions go through `changeMedicationStatus` or `removeMedication`, which write the change entry.
- **Change entries** (pure `medicationChange(before, after)`): create, a status change and an edit to a tracked field (name, strength, form, dose, directions, schedule) write one entry **in one transaction**; refill, reason, provider and type edits write none. Its id is `medicationChangeId(medicationId, changedAt)` (UUID v5), `changedAt` its `occurred_at`. The device writes both; the service rewrites the same entry from the op (`updated_at` as `changedAt`), so its PUT is an idempotent upsert.
- **Documents:** `note_document`'s `medication` subject (`medication_id`, a live one).
- **Sync:** in the `care_groups` stream and the publication (keep its migration's hand-written `ALTER PUBLICATION`); upload is `medicationOp` ([sync](sync.md)). Device `medications.sync.ts` (medication and change entry in one transaction); wording in pure `medicationDisplay.ts`.
- **Screens** (`GROUP_SECTIONS`, opening Schedule, claiming `/medications/*`):
  - **Three tabs** (layout `route.tsx`: `PageHeader` with Print and, for writers, the `+` menu Add (`useAddMenu`): Medication over the current tab, Pass unless 8 (live); then `TabNav`): `schedule/` (Today, the doses action), `list/` (allergies, search, `FilterMenu` (Status, Type), chips, the list; `?q=`, `?status=`, `?type=`), each with its loader, parents of the sheets (`new`, `:medicationId/edit`, `allergies`), and `passes/` (the rounds; editing a sheet in it; add is the child `passes/new`, `appUrls.newPass`, `newPassGuard` sends viewers and a full group to the tab). `medicationTabOf`; sheet helpers take the tab. The weekly sheet goes back to Schedule; a medication's page, its Edit, the list print to the list.
  - **The medication page** (`medications/:medicationId`, banded, [ui-kit](ui-kit.md#bands)): Allergies, Details, prescriber and pharmacy links, refills, open linked todos (`LinkedTodos band`), live notes (made on the first change), history. Missing or other-group ones 404.
  - **Add and edit** are sheets (`new`, `:medicationId/edit`): a new provider is a name until Save, then `newProviders` inserts it in the same transaction. `medicationSheets.ts` (`sheetTab`) guards send viewers and bad medications to the tab underneath; closing returns via `MEDICATION_SHEET_STATE`; `onlyMedicationSheetChanged` (same tab only) skips reloads.
  - The provider page has a "Medications" region (`ProviderMedications`); pure `medicationList.ts` filters the list.
- **Printable sheets:** the medication list (`print/`, pure `printSheet(...)`: current by type, then by name; stopped within 90 days with how and why) and the weekly bedside sheet (`print/week?start=YYYY-MM-DD`, `weeklySheet.ts`: seven days, default this Monday; a group per pass; landscape), both from the header's Print `Menu`.
- **What's due** (pure `dueDoses.ts` in `@caretook/data/schema` with `duePasses`, `givenAllWrites`, `zonedDay`; the source for every due-dose reader): `dueDoses({ medications, doses, changes, now, timeZone, overdueAfterMinutes = 60, windowMinutes = 120 })` expands yesterday's and today's slots (`zonedDay`: DST gaps forward, repeats first) through `scheduleHistory`, matching a day either side. A dose naming a pass or time its medication still has takes that name's nearest slot in the window (already answered: it answers nothing), else the open one on its day; any other, the nearest open slot in the window. States: upcoming, due, overdue, given, skipped, refused; only yesterday's open slots are missed. Current, live medications only, from when added or started. `doseWarnings`: `alreadyGiven`, `overMax`. `useDueDoses` reruns every 30 s, reads `changes` from two days back, `ready` once all answer.
- **By pass:** a slot is keyed by its pass when it has one, so a retimed pass keeps its answered doses. Doses carry optional `passId` and `passLogId` (a round's id). **`duePasses`** rolls slots into passes; `doseWarnings` stays per medication. **Given all** (`givenAllWrites`: open slots, one round, reusing a started one) writes in one transaction (`logDoses`); **Undo** removes those doses (`removeDoses`) via one shared offer (`passUndo.ts`, 10 s), `usePassLog`.
- **Dose ids:** a dose naming a slot gets `doseSlotIdFor` (UUID v5 of medication, `doseSlotDay`, slot, status), a round `passRoundId`; a writer goes random when the id is already where it writes, live or deleted (`claimDoseIds`, the server's `slotDoseId`). A dose naming a slot since removed keeps its id. **On upload** (`upsert`, before the update check, for any logger): onto another member's live row nothing changes; onto a row another deleted it returns as the uploader's, unless they deleted it or it's their own replay (same time).
- **Today** (the Schedule tab; `TodaySection`): each pass with a state badge; writers get Given all, one-tap **Given** (now, with `scheduledFor` and the dose), Skipped and Refused; as-needed rows ask before passing the maximum.
- **The Today card's medications row** (`TodayMedicationsRow`): pure `medicationStrip.ts` picks the pass (overdue, else due, else next; tomorrow's first if none today). Writers get a Dose sheet link (`?pass=`) and Given all while due; the sheet lists due passes, then slots, warning before saving ("Log anyway").
- **Pass logs on the timeline:** doses sharing a `passLogId` fold into one item per day at the earliest dose's time (`groupPassLogs` in `passLogs.ts`; `foldPassLogs` in `timeline/dayItems.ts`; paging never splits one, `wholeLogsPage`). Writers edit a round's doses on the **pass sheet** (`timeline/pass/:passLogId/edit`, `updateDoses`).
- **On the timeline:** both kinds are in `TIMELINE_KINDS` and the chips. Medications come from one live query over the window (`timelineMedicationsQuery`), never per row. A dose opens its edit sheet for writers; a change opens nothing, ⋯ Delete only (`openRule`).
- **The Dose sheet** (`timeline/new/dose`; `appUrls.newDose`): the amount follows the medication's dose until typed; a hidden `scheduledFor` comes from `?slot=HH:MM`. `newDoseGuard` sends viewers to the timeline and drops an unknown medication or bad slot. Its last option **"+ New medication"** (`new`) adds: name (`NameField`), dose, type, as needed (no maximum). `quickAddRows`/`logDoseOfNewMedication` write the medication, its "started" entry and the dose in one transaction, no warnings; then the timeline's `AddedMedicationToast` (`addedNotice.ts`).
- **Doses over a range:** `doseSlotsBetween(...)` (DST-safe; deleted medications never) and `missedDoseCounts(slots)`, used by visit prep. Each day reads through pure `scheduleHistory`, undoing later change entries (deleted ones too): the schedule at the day's end, a slot only if on it at its own moment, none while stopped. No entries or a gap: the present schedule. A time since dropped still names its slot within the window. `logDoses` is `doseRows` plus the write, so quick entry plans in its own transaction.
- **Tests:** component tests build on `medicationWorld.tsx` (`fixClock`/`clockAt`).
