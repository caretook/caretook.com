# Medications

Code: `packages/data/src/db/schema/medications/`; `apps/web/app/lib/medications/`; `routes/app/groups/medications/`, `medication/`; `components/medications/`.

- **Table** (`medication`): `group_id`, `recipient_id`, `schedule` jsonb, `type` (`prescription | otc | supplement | other`; nullable; required via `medicationFormContentZSchema`/`requireType`), `status` `current | completed | discontinued`, `stop_reason` (`sideEffects | ineffective | prescriberStopped | other`), refills, `prescriber_id` and `pharmacy_id` (composite FKs to `provider`, **`NO ACTION`**: no column-list `SET NULL` in Drizzle; providers soft-delete), `deleted_at`.
- **Schedule:** `{ type: "scheduled", slots: [{ passId, amount? } | { time, amount? }] }` (1-8 distinct slots) or `{ type: "asNeeded", maxPerDay? }` (1-24), **in the recipient's zone**. **Every read goes through `normalizeSchedule`** (also the old `{ times }`); writes use `slots` only.
- **Passes** (`medication_pass`; **Med times** in visible text, B-695; code, URLs, `give_pass` keep pass) a group's named slots (`name`, `time` `HH:MM` recipient zone; by time, then name). Live names unique per group (any case), at most 8. **Write through `medicationPassService`:** a retime writes one `medicationChange` entry (`passMoved`, id `passMovedId(passId, changedAt)`); a delete turns its slots into `{ time, amount }` in every live medication's schedule in the same transaction. The device (`medicationPasses.sync.ts`) does both; the service repeats them idempotently. **Defaults** (Morning 08:00, Evening 20:00): `addDefaults`, on every group create.
- **Allergies** (`recipient_allergy`; `severity` `mild | moderate | severe | unknown`; soft deletes), through `recipientAllergyService`. `care_recipient.no_known_allergies_at` records "No known allergies", cleared by an added allergy and refused with allergies on file (`hasAllergies`). Wording: `allergies.ts`; device: `allergies.sync.ts`. Managed on the Profile (`profile/allergies/`; `allergySuggestions.ts`); Medications shows `AllergiesRegion` read-only. `PrintAllergies` tops only the list print, then `PrintConditions` (active; none: omitted); `NameField` suggests (`lib/core/nameSuggestions.ts`) and warns on a match.
- **What it's for** (`medication_condition`, `medicationConditionService`; [profiles](profiles.md)): links to the recipient's conditions, beside the free-text reason. The For field (`ConditionsField`: active conditions, plus a resolved linked one) posts `conditionIds`; a save checks them, then writes both; a refusal writes nothing. The page's For row links each.
- **Status rules:** completed and discontinued need `stopped_on`, discontinued a `stop_reason`; current clears both, completed the reason. The prescriber must be a live clinician or practice, the pharmacy a live pharmacy. `changeMedicationStatus` and `removeMedication` write the change entry.
- **Change entries** (pure `medicationChange(before, after)`): create, a status change and an edit to a tracked field (name, strength, form, dose, directions, schedule) write one entry **in one transaction**; refill, reason, provider and type edits write none. Id: `medicationChangeId(medicationId, changedAt)` (UUID v5; `changedAt` is its `occurred_at`). The device writes both; the service upserts the entry from the op (`updated_at` as `changedAt`). `medicationService.create` takes a server-only `via` (create-only `MedicationCreateOptions`) onto the "started" entry.
- **Documents:** `note_document`'s `medication` subject (`medication_id`).
- **Sync:** in the `care_groups` stream and publication (keep its migration's hand-written `ALTER PUBLICATION`); upload is `medicationOp` ([sync](sync.md)). Device `medications.sync.ts` (medication and change entry together); wording `medicationDisplay.ts`.
- **Screens** (opens Schedule):
  - **Three tabs** (layout `route.tsx`: `PageHeader` with Print and, for writers, the `+` menu (`useAddMenu`): Medication, Med time unless 8; then `TabNav`): `schedule/` (Today), `list/` (allergies, `SearchFilterBar` (Status, Type; `?q=`, `?status=`, `?type=`), the list) (each with a loader; parents of the sheets `new`, `:medicationId/edit`, `allergies`), and `passes/` (the rounds; add is the child `passes/new`, `appUrls.newPass`, `newPassGuard` sends viewers and a full group to the tab). The weekly sheet returns to Schedule; a medication's page, its Edit and the list print to the list.
  - **The medication page** (`medications/:medicationId`, banded, [ui-kit](ui-kit.md#bands)): Allergies, Details, prescriber and pharmacy links, refills, open linked todos (`LinkedTodos band`), live notes (made on the first change), history. Back returns to its origin (`medicationOrigin.ts`), else the list. Missing or other-group: 404.
  - **Add and edit** are sheets (`new`, `:medicationId/edit`): a new provider is a name until Save, then `newProviders` inserts it in that transaction. The form is on `FormTabs`: Essentials (name, type, strength and dose on one row, schedule, prescriber, pharmacy), Details (form, directions, conditions, why, started on), Refills (refills left, Rx number, last filled, quantity); every type shows every field. At set times opens an overlay (a modal `<dialog>` in the form, `data-schedule-overlay`) and the form shows a `scheduleText` summary with Edit times; a refusal inside reopens it at the field (`revealInSchedule` after `revealField`). A new one starts As needed. Saving opens its page (`replace`; Back: the tab beneath); a conditions refusal after the write still opens it. `medicationSheets.ts` (`sheetTab`) guards send viewers and bad medications to the tab beneath; closing returns via `MEDICATION_SHEET_STATE`; `onlyMedicationSheetChanged` skips reloads.
  - The provider page's "Medications" region: `ProviderMedications`; `medicationList.ts` filters the list.
- **Printable sheets:** the medication list (`print/`, `printSheet`: current by type, then name; stopped within 90 days, how and why) and the weekly bedside sheet (`print/week?start=`, `weeklySheet.ts`: seven days from Monday; a group per pass; no allergies or conditions; each dose has G/S/R boxes (given, skipped, refused), initials and a legend).
- **What's due** (pure `dueDoses.ts` in `@caretook/data/schema`, with `zonedDay`; every reader uses it): `dueDoses({ medications, doses, changes, now, timeZone, overdueAfterMinutes = 60, windowMinutes = 120 })` expands yesterday's and today's slots (`zonedDay`: DST gaps forward, repeats first) through `scheduleHistory`, matching a day either side. A dose naming a pass or time its medication still has takes that name's nearest slot in the window (if answered, nothing), else the open one on its day; any other takes the nearest open one in the window. States: upcoming, due, overdue, given, skipped, refused; only yesterday's open slots miss. Only current, live medications. `doseWarnings`: `alreadyGiven`, `overMax`. `useDueDoses` reruns every 30 s.
- **By pass:** a slot is keyed by its pass when it has one, so a retimed pass keeps answered doses. Doses carry optional `passId` and `passLogId` (a round's id). `duePasses` rolls slots into passes. **Given all** (`givenAllWrites`: open slots, one round, reusing a started one) writes in one transaction (`logDoses`); **Undo** removes them (`removeDoses`): one shared 10 s toast (`passUndo.ts`, `offerUndoToast` under `UndoToastProvider`).
- **Dose ids:** a dose naming a slot gets `doseSlotIdFor` (UUID v5 of medication, `doseSlotDay`, slot, status), a round `passRoundId`; a writer goes random if the id exists where it writes (`claimDoseIds`, the server's `slotDoseId`). **On upload** (`upsert`, before the update check, per-id lock): every accepted upload is noted in `dose_duplicate_upload`. Onto another member's live row nothing changes, nor does a PUT already deleted or the device's PATCH of its copy (`OWN_DOSE_COPY_METADATA`); onto a row another deleted it returns as the uploader's, unless they deleted it, it's their replay or a noted retry.
- **Today** (the Schedule tab; `TodaySection`): each pass with a state badge; writers get Given all, one-tap **Given**, Skipped, Refused; as-needed rows ask before passing the maximum. Passes shows today's state and Given all. Double doses (`DoseSlot.extra`, `AsNeededToday.extra`) show `ExtraDoseWarning`.
- **The Today card's medications row** (`TodayMedicationsRow`): `medicationStrip.ts` picks the pass (overdue, else due, else next; tomorrow's first). Writers get a Dose sheet link (`?pass=`) and Given all while due; the sheet lists due passes, then slots, warning "Log anyway".
- **Pass logs on the timeline:** doses sharing a `passLogId` fold into one item per day, at the earliest dose's time (`groupPassLogs`, `foldPassLogs`; paging never splits one). Writers edit a round on the **pass sheet** (`timeline/pass/:passLogId/edit`, `updateDoses`).
- **On the timeline:** both kinds are in `TIMELINE_KINDS` and the chips; one live window query. Writers edit a dose; a change only ⋯ Delete.
- **The Dose sheet** (`timeline/new/dose`; `appUrls.newDose`): the amount follows the medication's dose until typed; hidden `scheduledFor` comes from `?slot=HH:MM`. `newDoseGuard` sends viewers to the timeline, dropping an unknown medication or slot. Its last option **"+ New medication"** (`new`) adds name (`NameField`; US brands in parentheses: "Tylenol" finds "Acetaminophen (Tylenol)"), dose, type, as needed (no maximum). `quickAddRows`/`logDoseOfNewMedication` write the medication, its "started" entry and the dose in one transaction; then `AddedMedicationToast` (`addedNotice.ts`).
- **Doses over a range:** `doseSlotsBetween(...)` (DST-safe) and `missedDoseCounts(slots)` (visit prep); `doseSlotsBetween` takes an optional `now` (states settle there); `doseRecord` (pure): past rounds, slot states, doses, for `past_doses`; `sheetDoseOutcomes` (same file) decides each sheet mark for `log_past_doses`; `doseSchedule` (days ahead as rounds, no doses) for `dose_schedule`. Each day reads through `scheduleHistory`, undoing later change entries: the schedule at the day's end, a slot only if on it at its own moment, none while stopped; no entries or a gap: the present schedule.
- **Tests:** component tests use `medicationWorld.tsx`.
