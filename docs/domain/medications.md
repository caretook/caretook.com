# Medications

The medications area's rules and decisions. An orchestrator folds a landing's lasting decisions for this area in here.

PRD: [`plans/medications-prd.md`](../../plans/medications-prd.md).

Code: schema `packages/data/src/db/schema/medications/` (and `profiles/recipientAllergy.ts`); logic `apps/web/app/lib/medications/`; screens `routes/app/groups/medications/` and `medication/`; `components/medications/`.

- **Table** (`medication`): `group_id`, `recipient_id` (composite FK to `care_recipient`), the fields of a medication (`schedule` jsonb, `status` `current | completed | discontinued`, `stop_reason` `sideEffects | ineffective | prescriberStopped | other`, refills), `prescriber_id` and `pharmacy_id` (composite FKs to `provider`, **`NO ACTION`**: Drizzle 0.45 can't express a column-list `SET NULL`, and providers are only soft-deleted), `deleted_at`. Any member reads, writers write, delete is soft.
- **Schedule:** `{ type: "scheduled", slots: [{ passId, amount? } | { time, amount? }] }` (1-8 slots, no repeated pass or time) or `{ type: "asNeeded", maxPerDay? }` (1-24), **in the recipient's time zone**. **Every read goes through `normalizeSchedule`**, which also accepts the old `{ times }`; writes use `slots` only. Missed doses are worked out, never written.
- **Passes** (`medication_pass`): a group's named rounds (`name`, `time` `HH:MM` in the recipient's zone, `sort_order`, soft delete). Live names are unique per group, case-insensitive, at most 8 live. **Write through `medicationPassService`:** a retime writes one `medicationChange` entry with the `passMoved` variant (id `passMovedId(passId, changedAt)`), and a delete turns its slots into `{ time, amount }` in every live medication's schedule in the same transaction, with no change entries. The device (`medicationPasses.sync.ts`) does both in one local transaction; the service repeats them idempotently on upload. **Default passes** (Morning 08:00, Evening 20:00) come from `medicationPassService.addDefaults`, called by every group-create path; no backfill. Writers manage passes offline.
- **Allergies** (`recipient_allergy`; `severity` `mild | moderate | severe | unknown`; soft deletes), through `recipientAllergyService`. `care_recipient.no_known_allergies_at` records "No known allergies" and is cleared when an allergy is added; recording it with allergies on file is refused (`hasAllergies`). Wording and matching in `allergies.ts`, device in `allergies.sync.ts`. `AllergiesRegion` shows them, `PrintAllergies` tops both printed sheets, and `NameField` warns on a name match. Editing is writers-only.
- **Status rules:** completed and discontinued need `stopped_on`; discontinued needs `stop_reason`; current clears the stop fields; completed clears the reason. The prescriber must be a live clinician or practice in the group and the pharmacy a live pharmacy, checked only when set or changed. Status actions go through `changeMedicationStatus` or `removeMedication`, so the change entry is written with them.
- **Change entries** (`medicationChange(before, after)`, pure): create, a status change and an edit to a tracked field (name, strength, form, dose, directions, schedule) write one `medicationChange` entry **in the same transaction**; refill, reason and provider edits write none. Its id is `medicationChangeId(medicationId, changedAt)` (UUID v5); `changedAt` is also its `occurred_at`. The device writes the medication, then the entry; on upload the service writes the same entry from the medication op (the device's `updated_at` as `changedAt`), so the entry's own PUT is an idempotent upsert.
- **Documents:** `note_document`'s `medication` subject with `medication_id`; `noteDocumentService.create` accepts a medication that isn't deleted.
- **Sync:** `medication` is in the `care_groups` stream and the publication (the hand-written `ALTER PUBLICATION` at the end of its migration: keep it when regenerating); upload is `medicationOp` ([sync](sync.md)).
- **Device** (`medications.sync.ts`): queries, `dayInZone`/`dayBounds` (the recipient's day, DST-safe) and local writes, each medication write with its change entry in one transaction. Wording is pure (`medicationDisplay.ts`).
- **Screens** (a Medications entry in `GROUP_SECTIONS` after Providers, claiming `/medications/*`; URLs through `appUrls`):
  - **The medication page** (`medications/:medicationId`): details, prescriber and pharmacy as provider links (greyed "(removed)" when deleted), its live notes (created on the first real change; read-only for viewers), open linked tasks (`LinkedTasks`, see [tasks](tasks.md)), and the history of doses and changes. Missing, deleted or other-group medications are the 404.
  - **Add and edit** are sheet routes (`new`, `:medicationId/edit`): a new provider is held as a name until Save, then `newProviders` inserts it first, in the medication's transaction. The guards (`medicationSheets.ts`) send viewers and missing, deleted or other-group medications to the list; closing goes back through `MEDICATION_SHEET_STATE`.
  - The provider page has a "Medications" region (`ProviderMedications`); the list filters through the pure `medicationList.ts` (`?q=`, `?status=`).
- **Printable sheets** (local data only, so they work offline): the medication list (`print/`, pure `printSheet(...)`: current by name; stopped within 90 days with how and why; the day in the recipient's zone) and the weekly bedside sheet (`print/week?start=YYYY-MM-DD`, `weeklySheet.ts`: seven days from the start, default this week's Monday; a group per pass; landscape through `@page weekly`, opted in with `[page:weekly]`), both from the list's Print `Menu`. Print CSS (`@media print` in `app.css`) forces black on white; the `/app` header and tab bar carry `print:hidden`.
- **What's due** (`dueDoses.ts`, pure; the one source for Today, the Today card and the Dose sheet): `dueDoses({ medications, doses, now, timeZone, overdueAfterMinutes = 60, windowMinutes = 120 })` expands yesterday's and today's slots in the recipient's zone (`lib/core/zonedDay.ts`: a skipped time moves forward, a repeated one is the first). A dose with `scheduledFor` answers the nearest slot with that time and never another; without one it answers the nearest open slot within the window. States: upcoming, due, overdue, given, skipped, refused; only yesterday's open slots are missed. Only current, live medications, none before they were added or started. `doseWarnings` gives `alreadyGiven` and `overMax`. `useDueDoses` (`dueDoses.sync.ts`) re-evaluates every 30 s and stays not-`ready` until its queries answer.
- **By pass:** a slot is keyed by its pass when it has one, so a retimed pass keeps its answered doses; a dose answers a pass slot by `passId` first, then `scheduledFor`. Doses carry optional `passId` and `passLogId` (one round's shared id). **`duePasses`** rolls slots into passes; Today, the strip, the Dose sheet and Upcoming read it, while `doseWarnings` stays per medication. **Given all** (`givenAllWrites`: open slots only, one round, reusing a round already started) writes in one local transaction (`logDoses`); **Undo** removes exactly those doses (`removeDoses`) through one shared offer (`passUndo.ts`, 10 s) and `usePassLog`.
- **Today** (top of the list; `TodaySection`): each pass with a state badge; writers get Given all, one-tap **Given** (logged now with `scheduledFor` and the medication's dose), Skipped and Refused; as-needed rows ask before going past the maximum.
- **The Today card's medications row** (`TodayMedicationsRow`, [timeline](timeline.md)): the pure `medicationStrip.ts` picks the pass (overdue, else due, else next; tomorrow's first when today has none). Writers get a link to the Dose sheet on that pass (`?pass=`) and Given all while due. The Dose sheet lists due passes, then due slots (a slot posts as `medicationId="<id>@HH:MM"`), and warns before saving ("Log anyway").
- **Pass logs on the timeline:** within a day, doses sharing a `passLogId` fold into one item at the earliest dose's time (`groupPassLogs` in `passLogs.ts`; `foldPassLogs` in `timeline/dayItems.ts` runs after the care period markers; paging never splits a log, `wholeLogsPage`). Writers open the **pass sheet** (`timeline/pass/:passLogId/edit`) to edit every dose together (`updateDoses`); viewers go to the timeline.
- **On the timeline:** both kinds are in `TIMELINE_KINDS` and the kind chips. The medications come from one live query over the window (`timelineMedicationsQuery`), never one per row. A dose opens its edit sheet for writers; a medication change opens nothing and its ⋯ is Delete only (`openRule`, `entryActionItems`, `isSystemWrittenKind`).
- **The Dose sheet** (`timeline/new/dose`; `appUrls.newDose`): the amount follows the medication's dose until typed; a hidden `scheduledFor` comes from `?slot=HH:MM`. `newDoseGuard` sends viewers to the timeline and drops an unknown medication or bad slot. Editing a dose is the same sheet with the medication fixed.
- **Doses over a range:** `doseSlotsBetween(...)` (DST-safe; a stopped medication counts to its `stoppedOn`, deleted ones not at all) and `missedDoseCounts(slots)`, used by visit prep. `logDoses` is `doseRows` (check and plan) plus the write, so quick entry's runner plans a pass's rows inside its own transaction.
- **Seeds** (`packages/data/src/db/seeds/medications.ts`, `medicationPasses.ts`): both groups have the default passes and America/Los_Angeles recipients; seeded doses fill the timeline's first page, so filter by kind to find older entries.
- **Tests:** component tests build on `medicationWorld.tsx` (`fixClock`/`clockAt`; fixture columns under `.row`); `syncMedications.int.test.ts` covers an offline add with a new prescriber, edit and discontinue in one batch.
