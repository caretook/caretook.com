# Providers

- **Tables:**
  - `provider`: `group_id`, `type` (`clinician | practice | pharmacy | other`), `name`, `specialty`, `practice_id`, `phones` jsonb `[{ label, number }]` (at most 6), `email`, `address`, `website`, `status` (`current | past`), **`is_primary`** (not `primary`, an SQL keyword in Postgres, SQLite and PowerSync), `deleted_at`. Unique `(group_id, id)`.
  - `timeline_entry_provider`: a link from a note or event to a provider. Its id is `entryProviderId(entryId, providerId)`, a UUID v5 (`db/schema/core/uuidV5.ts`), so the device and server derive the same id and a pair is one row. Composite FKs cascade; unlink is a hard delete.
- **Rules** (`providerService`, `entryProviderService`; write through them):
  - A practice must be a live practice or pharmacy in the same group, never the provider itself; practices and pharmacies have none. While live providers name one, it can't become a clinician or `other` (`providerService:practiceInUse`) or be deleted (`practiceHasProviders`, 409; `FOR UPDATE` here, `FOR SHARE` in `checkPractice`); the device refuses both too, and the delete confirm counts them and disables Delete. Practice ↔ pharmacy is allowed.
  - Delete is soft and hides a provider from lists. Lists are in display order: primary, then current by name, then past by name.
  - Links go only to live notes and events in the same group; a deleted provider is refused; an id in another group is `unauthorized`; unlinking a missing link succeeds (replays).
- **Device** (`lib/providers/providers.sync.ts`): queries (the list, one provider, an entry's providers including deleted ones for greyed names, a provider's entries, link history) and local writes (create, update, soft remove, link, unlink, and create-and-link in one transaction). Linking needs the entry stored locally, so a draft is materialized first. A device-created provider document writes `subject: "provider"` and `provider_id`.
- **Seeds** (`seeds/providers.ts`): six fictional providers for Dev Family, one for Dev Family 2.
- **Screens** (children of the group route, built through `appUrls.providers`, `newProvider`, `editProvider`, `provider`; a Providers entry in `GROUP_SECTIONS`, lucide `Stethoscope`):
  - **The list** (`routes/app/groups/providers/`): live from local data. Search (`?q=`; every word in the name, specialty or practice name, folded), a `FilterMenu` beside it, one Type section (`?type=`, carrying the box's live text), all in the URL. Past providers always list under their label. **Condensed** (default) or **Full**, saved per user as `userPreferences.providersView` (apart from `timelineView`), from a View options menu sharing `components/settings/LayoutChoice.tsx` with the timeline. Condensed rows are one line with one contact action (call, else email, else maps); Full rows carry no contact details. Rows open the provider page; writers get Add.
  - **Contact actions** (pure `lib/providers/contactActions.ts`; `components/ContactActions.tsx`): `tel:` keeps digits and a leading `+`, an extension after a pause (`x214` → `,214`); maps is a Google Maps search; websites get `https://`, http(s) only.
  - **Sheets** `providers/new` (optional `?type=`) and `providers/:providerId/edit`, children of the list (routing's sheet pattern; `providerSheetGuard.ts`): `ProviderForm` with type, name, specialty, practice (clinicians and other contacts), up to 6 phones (the label a native select: blank, Office, Mobile, Front desk, Nurse line, After hours, Refills, Fax, Custom, which reveals a text input, limit 40; recipient and user contact forms keep a text input), email, address, website, primary and past; field messages from the shared Zod schema. Saving a new provider opens its page with `replace` (Back goes to the directory); the route returns `CreateResult` (`lib/core/api.ts`). Writes are local (`createProvider`, `updateProvider`, `removeProvider`) and upload later; delete is soft, after a `ConfirmDialog`. Viewers and missing providers get the list.
  - **The provider page** (`routes/app/groups/provider/`, `providers/:providerId`, banded, [ui-kit](ui-kit.md#bands)): a `DetailHeader` (the practice a link in its details; Edit and Copy to another group), then Contact, Visits, Todos, the live notes in `LiveNotesEditor` (created on the first real change through `createProviderDocument`; read-only for viewers). **Copy to another group** (`CopyToGroup`, writers who write in another group): a `ConfirmDialog` picking the target, the notes rule, `importProviders`; then "Copied to X." or "Already in X. Nothing was copied.". Missing, deleted or other-group providers are the 404.
- **Conditions** (a provider can diagnose or manage a condition; [profiles](profiles.md)): the provider page's `ProviderConditions` lists them live, active first, marked Diagnosed and/or Manages, each opening its sheet over the profile; none, no section. A pharmacy can't diagnose or manage (`fitsCondition`). Shared pickers (`ProviderPickerField`, `ProvidersPickerField`; `components/providers/`) take a `fits` filter and can add the typed name, written with the form's row when it saves. The pure rules are in `lib/providers/providerPicker.ts` (`pickerOptions`: every typed word matched, at most `PICKER_LIMIT`; `canAddPicked`; `chosenLabel`: "(removed)", "(new)").
- **Linking from notes and events:**
  - **The With row** (`EntryWithRow` in `routes/app/groups/entry/`, `EntryHeader`'s optional `with` region below title and time): linked providers as chips (`entryProvidersQuery`) opening their pages, a deleted one greyed "(removed)". Writers get × and Add: a `ComboBox` (`pickerOptions` in `withProviders.ts`: live, not linked, the directory's matching, up to 20) with "Add "<typed>" as a provider" (a clinician, created and linked at once). On a draft, `ensureStored()` runs first. Viewers see chips only.
  - **Mentions** (`@` in any `LiveNotesEditor` given `providers`; node in [live-notes](live-notes.md)): the popup is `mentionChoices` (`lib/providers/mentionOptions.ts`): this group's live providers by the directory's matching and order, up to 20, linked ones included; then "From <group>" (below); then, for writers, 'Add "<typed>" as a provider' unless a live provider has that folded name. The query allows spaces (`mentionQueryOpen` closes it at a third word matching no one). On an entry a pick links like the With row (`ensureStored()` first; idempotent) and the add row is `createAndLinkProvider`; elsewhere a mention is only a chip and the add row is `createProvider`. Deleting a mention keeps the link. Chips (`MentionChip`) show the current name and open the page; a deleted or unknown one is greyed. Read through `useMentionProviders`.
  - **Suggestions** (pure `lib/providers/providerSuggestions.ts`, `suggestProviders`; read locally by `lib/providers/providerSuggestions.sync.ts`, so they work offline): while nothing is linked, writers see up to 5 chips ("Suggested providers"), linked through the same path. Ranked by names in the title, then the text (whole words folded, honorifics ignored; clinicians also by a last name of 3+ letters), then links on earlier entries with the same title, then recent links (14-day half-life). Current providers only; the title being typed counts.
  - **The timeline's "with …" line** (`EntryBody`): up to 2 names then "+N", removed greyed, text only; one live query (`timelineLinksQuery`) over the entries' window, never one per row. Wording: `lib/providers/entryProviders.ts` (`chipLabel`, `withSummary`, `withText`, `groupLinksByEntry`).
  - **The provider page's "Linked entries"** (`LinkedEntries`, `providerEntriesQuery`): newest first.
- **Importing** (a copy, not a share): new ids through ordinary local creates (`importProviders`, one transaction) that upload as provider creates, so the server's checks apply. No server path or record of the source; notes, links and todos stay behind ("Provider notes stay in <group>"). For writers here who belong to the source. The pure `planImport` (`lib/providers/providerImport.ts`) copies type, name, specialty, phones, email, address, website and status, primary off; skips "Already here" (a live provider of the same type and `foldName`); maps a practice to a live one of the same type and name, else copies it once; writes practices first; a result per chosen provider. Wording in `importWording.ts`; groups are named by the recipient's display name, else the group name. Three ways in:
  - **the import sheet** `providers/import` (`appUrls.importProviders`, `importProvidersGuard`): icon-only "Import from another group" before the header's `+` ("Add provider"); pick the group, tick providers, "Import N", a status with the counts; its guard counts only ticks;
  - **Copy to another group** on the provider page (above);
  - **"From <group>"** in the With picker (`ComboBox` `sections`) and the `@` popup (`lib/providers/fromGroups.ts`, read by `otherGroupProviders.sync.ts`): once something is typed, up to 5 live matches per group not already here; a pick copies (with its practice) and on an entry links in one transaction (`importAndLinkProvider`), elsewhere only mentions.
- **Tests:** no area check. Component tests share `providers/providerWorld.tsx` (a directory shaped like the seeds, a second group, `PROVIDER_ROUTES`): the directory (`providers/providers.browser.test.tsx`), the sheets and the device module's refusals (`providerSheets`), a provider's page (`provider/provider.browser.test.tsx`), the entry page's With row and suggestions (`entry/entryWithRow.browser.test.tsx`) and mentions (`entry/entryMentions.browser.test.tsx`); importing is in `providers.browser.test.tsx` and `syncProviders.int.test.ts`, as are two devices uploading one link. Cross-device: the sync matrix, `live-notes`.
- **Prepare for a visit** (writers, the provider page's `ProviderVisitPrep`): prepares the provider's next appointment and opens it, or offers to create one; rules in [appointments](appointments.md).
- **Linked todos** (tasks PRD): the provider page lists its open linked todos after visit prep (`LinkedTodos` from `components/todos`), with "Add todo" prefilled with `providerId`; see [todos](todos.md).
