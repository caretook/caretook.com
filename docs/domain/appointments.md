# Appointments

PRD: [`plans/appointments-prd.md`](../../plans/appointments-prd.md); repeating ones: [`appointments-2`](../../plans/appointments-2-prd.md). **No new kind:** a future event is an appointment, and "upcoming" is worked out, never stored. **No reminders** (`planner-2`, B-248): later things are [todos](todos.md).

- **Columns on `timeline_entry`** (migration `0015_appointments`): `ends_at` (events; after `occurred_at`; null when all-day), `all_day` (notes and events; `occurred_at` is the start of the day in `time_zone`), `place` (events), `cancelled_at`, `cancelled_by` (set by the server, audit-style), `cancel_reason` (events), and `rescheduled_to` (events, only when cancelled): a composite FK with `ON DELETE SET NULL ("rescheduled_to")`, **hand-edited** (Drizzle can't express it); keep when regenerating. Other kinds refuse these fields (`db/schema/core/entryTiming.ts`); a `rescheduled_to` in another group is `timelineEntryService:rescheduledToNotInGroup`. Synced (`all_day` as 1/0).
- **Future dates:** only events may be ahead (`FUTURE_KINDS`, `FUTURE_KINDS_SQL`); other kinds are refused past now + 5 min on the device, now + 1 h on the server ("Can't be in the future"), notes only on create or a moved time (a stored future note stays editable, hidden until its time). Time fields max at now; `?when=pick` and `newPlannerEntry` are events only.
- **Time rules** (pure, tested, one home): `lib/appointments/upcoming.ts` (`entryTiming`: past, upcoming or now; all-day lasts to the end of its day in its zone; `upcomingOrder` soonest first, all-day first in a day; `upcomingOrderIn(zone)`/`listedDay` for lists grouped by the viewer's day; `nextUp` skips cancelled; `historyCutoff`), `upcomingDays.ts` (`upcomingSections`, "Now · until …" first; `pastSections`), `upcomingLabels.ts` (`timeRange`, `nowLabel`, `cancelledLabel`, `rescheduledLabel`, `nextStripText`). The history, upcoming (90 days) and past windows share `entryWindow`'s filter conditions.
- **The entry page:** the time region shows the range or "All day"; writers' time chip edits the end time (events) and all-day through `components/schedule`'s `ScheduleFields` (rules in `lib/appointments/entrySchedule.ts`). Events get a place row (`lib/appointments/entryPlace.ts`: empty shows the first linked provider's address, greyed "from <provider>"; either has a map action). `entries/new?kind=…&when=pick` (`WHEN_PARAM`, `WHEN_PICK`) opens the picker first, at 9:00 once a day is picked.
- **The Planner** (`routes/app/groups/planner/`, `appUrls.planner`; after Medications, see [app-shell](app-shell.md)): live, by day soonest first; event cards: the time above the title, Call and Directions (`upcomingContact.ts`) and the author in a footer; todo rows keep `[data-when-column]`. `PlannerHeader`: `PageHeader` "Planner", for writers a + Menu "Add" (Event via `appUrls.newPlannerEntry`; Todo, whose save lands on Todos and whose cancel returns to its tab; a title-less item reads "Event"), and `TabNav` Upcoming | Past | [Todos](todos.md), each carrying its filters. On Upcoming a `FilterMenu` beside the tabs, "Show": All, Events (`?kind=event`), Todos (`?show=todos`; `upcomingFilters`), with `FilterMenuChips` and an uncounted "By <name>" author chip. `planner/past` (`plannerPast`) lists past events newest first, cancelled included, paged; no Filter button.
- **Todos in the Planner** (`lib/todos/plannerTodos.ts`: `plannerDayTrees`, `withDueTodos`, `showsTodos`): one item per day tree (`todoTreesQuery`, `todoDayTrees`) in the 90-day window, placed and ranked by its lead (the top todo on its day, else the step due then; overdue under Today); `TodoTree` with the top row in `TodoRow`'s `card` look, steps indented, a quick add per row for writers; a tick (`TodoUndoStatus`) under All and Todos (`?show=todos`), hidden under Events or an author filter. Tag and text filters match, ancestors as context. No entries, doses, passes.
- **Cancel** (events, writers, past ones too): Cancel or Undo cancel before Delete in `entryActionItems`, a `CancelEventDialog` with an optional reason, a local write (`cancelEvent`, `uncancelEvent` in `lib/appointments/appointments.sync.ts`; `lib/appointments/eventCancel.ts`). Cancelled events are struck through and labelled everywhere, never "next", and the entry page shows a banner (reason, by whom, when; Undo for writers).
- **Back to the opener** (B-690 `plannerReturn.ts`; B-936 `entryOrigin.ts`): Add > Event, Planner rows, a provider's linked entries and visit prep, and a todo's links pass the opener in link state (kept over the first save's replace); Back and delete return there (a history step if just behind). A new Planner event returns to Upcoming or Past (`isUpcoming`). Else Timeline.
- **Reschedule** (`RescheduleSheet`, opened in place from the ⋯ menu on the timeline, the Planner and the entry page; no URL): "Keep the old one as cancelled" is on by default for an upcoming event (`lib/appointments/eventReschedule.ts`). Off is an ordinary time update. On is **one local transaction** (`rescheduleEvent`): the new event, its provider links, its document seeded by one `note_update` with the old document's full Yjs state, its live todos' links (open and done) moved over, and the old event cancelled with `rescheduled_to`; then the new event opens. A series visit moved on its own moves them too; a plain time change keeps them. The old event's page shows "Rescheduled to <day>", notes read-only. `removeEntry` clears `rescheduled_to` on events pointing at the deleted one (the server's FK does the same).
- **Visit prep** (B-58; `visitPrep.ts`, pure; `components/visitPrep/`): `visitWindow` starts at the last past, not cancelled event linked to the providers, else 30 days back; `visitPrepSection` lists medication changes (pass retimes included) and the current list, each vital's range, average and direction against the same length before, symptoms with counts, highest severity and "new", events (not cancelled, not the last visit or this one), overlapping care-period stays; empty ones omitted. "Missed doses" follows Current medications (`missedDoseCounts`). Entry points (writers): "Prepare for this visit" on an upcoming event's page; "Prepare for a visit" on a provider's page (their next appointment, or `appUrls.newAppointmentWith`, which links them); Prepare in Planner rows' ⋯. Each opens `VisitPrepSheet` (start editable, ≤ now; preview; Insert). `entryActionItems` order: edit, prepare, reschedule, cancel/uncancel, print, delete. An event's page has the Todos section ([todos](todos.md)); removing an event or series keeps its todos and links (readers skip it).
- **Repeating** (B-60; `0029_appointments_calendars`): `appointment_series` (synced; writers; `kind`: Zod takes `event` only, `topUp` skips stored note series; `rule` RRULE text with DAILY, WEEKLY or MONTHLY, INTERVAL, BYDAY (an ordinal for monthly, kept when edited), BYMONTHDAY (-1: the last day), COUNT or UNTIL; `starts_on`, `start_time` (null all-day), `time_zone`, the template `title`, `duration_minutes`, `place`, `provider_ids`; `topped_up_through`; soft delete). `timeline_entry` gains `series_id` (composite FK, `ON DELETE SET NULL`, **hand-edited** like `rescheduled_to`, plus the `ALTER PUBLICATION`: keep both), `series_day` and `series_detached`, unique `(series_id, series_day)` among live rows. A **visit is an ordinary entry** with id UUID v5 of (series, day) (`seriesVisitId`), so the device and server converge; the first keeps the draft's id and notes. `seriesVisits` (rrule 2.8.1, `zonedInstant` places floating time) is only in `@caretook/data/series` (with `seriesEdit`): `budget.json` keeps rrule out of first load. `repeatText` says a rule in words; the preview shows a timed visit's day and time in the viewer's zone (B-728); `createSeries` (`series.sync.ts`) writes the series and 90 days of visits in one local transaction. **The server writes visits only for a series without `toppedUpThrough`**, and `appointmentSeriesService.topUp` (the calendar cron's end) keeps series 90 days ahead from max(`topped_up_through` + 1, its zone's today), reading only due ones, never rewriting ended ones (B-311), skipping taken days, past failing ones; a taken id gets `seriesVisitFallbackId`. Batched inserts (B-755).
- **Editing a series** (`planSeriesEdit`, pure, `seriesEdit.ts`; `applySeriesEdit` on the device, one transaction): scope **only this visit** changes it and detaches it (cancel and reschedule detach too; later series edits leave it); **this and later** splits: the old series ends the day before (UNTIL, or is deleted when it is the first visit), a new one starts on this day, and later visits move onto it in place (same ids, notes, todos) when still in the rule, else are deleted unless they have notes, a live linked todo or a cancel (then detached). It takes time of day and length only (a day change goes through the rule). Past visits (`isPastVisit` on both sides: all-day lasts its day) are never written; "this and later" isn't offered on a started visit (`timelineEntryService:pastVisit`, 409). A split uploads the new series, the old PATCH, entry changes, then new visits. UI: `SeriesScopeDialog` (a `ChoiceDialog`; says "repeating event": `scopeQuestion(action)`) and `useSeriesScope` (`ONLY_THIS_VISIT_STATE`), an `editSeries` item in `entryActionItems`, `SeriesEditSheet`, `RepeatField` and `RepeatSheet` (`components/repeat/`), `RepeatMark` on rows and the Today card; the entry page asks the scope on title, time and place. Calendars: [calendars](calendars.md).
- **Seeds** (`seeds/appointments.ts`, own ids, placed by `daysAhead`): about eight upcoming items for Dev Family (providers and places, one all-day, one cancelled, a rescheduled pair; no future note or note series), two past cancelled events; one for Dev Family 2.
- **Tests:** Component tests, fixed clock (`fixClock()`, `planner/plannerWorld.tsx`; device-clock times): `planner` (tabs, filters, no doses), `plannerTodos`, `plannerEvents` (cancel, Undo, reschedule), `plannerAddTodo`, `plannerEventReturn`, `timelineStrips`. `plannerSeries`, `plannerRemind` ("Remind me"). `roundtrip` 6a: reschedule end to end (new event, link, notes, the old one cancelled) and a series split.
