# Appointments

The appointments area's rules and decisions, moved out of AGENTS.md (whose core rules still apply). An orchestrator folds a landing's lasting decisions for this area in here.

PRD: [`plans/appointments-prd.md`](../../plans/appointments-prd.md). **No new kind:** a future event is an appointment, a future note a reminder, and "upcoming" is worked out, never stored.

- **Columns on `timeline_entry`** (migration `0015_appointments`): `ends_at` (events; after `occurred_at`; null when all-day), `all_day` (notes and events; `occurred_at` is the start of the day in `time_zone`), `place` (events), `cancelled_at`, `cancelled_by` (set by the server, audit-style), `cancel_reason` (events), and `rescheduled_to` (events, only when cancelled): a composite FK to `(group_id, id)` with `ON DELETE SET NULL ("rescheduled_to")`, **hand-edited** in the SQL (Drizzle can't express a column list): keep it when regenerating. Other kinds refuse these fields (`db/schema/core/entryTiming.ts`); a `rescheduled_to` in another group is `timelineEntryService:rescheduledToNotInGroup`. They're in the stream query, the device schema and the upload switch (`all_day` as 1/0).
- **Future dates:** only notes and events may be in the future. Symptoms, vitals, doses and medication changes are refused beyond now + 5 min on the device and now + 1 h on the server (the issue is on `occurredAt`: "Can't be in the future"); the symptom and vital sheets' `DateTimeField` has a maximum of now.
- **Time rules** (pure, tested, one home): `lib/appointments/upcoming.ts` (`entryTiming`: past, upcoming or now; an all-day item lasts to the end of its day in its own zone; `upcomingOrder` soonest first, all-day first in a day; `nextUp` skips cancelled; `historyCutoff`), `lib/appointments/upcomingDays.ts` (`upcomingSections` with "Now · until …" first, `pastSections`), `lib/appointments/upcomingLabels.ts` (`timeRange`, `nowLabel`, `cancelledLabel`, `rescheduledLabel`, `nextStripText`), and "Tomorrow" in `dayLabel`. The history, upcoming (90 days) and past windows share `entryWindow`'s filter conditions.
- **The entry page:** the time region shows the range or "All day"; writers' time chip edits the end time (events) and all-day through `components/schedule`'s `ScheduleFields` (rules in `lib/appointments/entrySchedule.ts`). Events get a place row (`lib/appointments/entryPlace.ts`: empty shows the first linked provider's address, greyed "from <provider>"; either has a map action). `entries/new?kind=…&when=pick` (`WHEN_PARAM`, `WHEN_PICK` in `appUrls`) opens the picker first, at 9:00 once a day is picked.
- **The Upcoming section** (`routes/app/groups/upcoming/`, `appUrls.upcoming`, a `GROUP_SECTIONS` entry after Medications, lucide `CalendarClock`): live, by day soonest first, with kind and author chips in the URL; rows reuse `EntryBody` with call and map actions (`upcomingContact.ts`). Writers get Add appointment and Add reminder (`?when=pick`). `upcoming/past` lists past events newest first, cancelled included, paged.
- **Due doses in Upcoming** (`lib/appointments/upcomingDoses.ts`, over `medications`' `dueDoses`): today's open slots (upcoming, due, overdue) are merged under their day in time order (`withDueDoses`; due and overdue under Today, never "Now"), shown by `UpcomingDoseRow` with a Due or Overdue badge. Writers' rows open the Dose sheet on that slot (with `SHEET_LINK_STATE` from `lib/timeline/timelineSheet.ts`, so it comes back to Upcoming); viewers' have no link. Never written as entries; hidden under a kind or author filter. Only the section shows them, not the Next strip or block.
- **Tasks in Upcoming** (`lib/tasks/upcomingTasks.ts`: `withDueTasks`, `showsTasks`; tasks PRD): open tasks with a due day in the 90-day window are merged after due doses, under their day in time order (overdue ones under Today), shown by `UpcomingTaskRow` with a `TaskTick` (and `TaskUndoStatus`); hidden under a kind or author filter. Never written as entries.
- **Cancel** (events, writers, past ones too): Cancel or Undo cancel before Delete in `entryActionItems`, a `CancelEventDialog` with an optional reason, a local write (`cancelEvent`, `uncancelEvent` in `lib/appointments/appointments.sync.ts`; `lib/appointments/eventCancel.ts`). Cancelled events are struck through and labelled everywhere, never "next", and the entry page shows a banner (reason, by whom, when; Undo for writers).
- **Reschedule** (`RescheduleSheet`, opened in place from the ⋯ menu on the timeline, Upcoming and the entry page; no URL): "Keep the old one as cancelled" is on by default for an upcoming event (`lib/appointments/eventReschedule.ts`). Off is an ordinary time update. On is **one local transaction** (`rescheduleEvent`): the new event, its provider links, its document seeded by one `note_update` with the old document's full Yjs state, and the old event cancelled with `rescheduled_to`; then the new event opens. The old event's page shows "Rescheduled to <day>" with its notes read-only. `removeEntry` clears `rescheduled_to` on events pointing at the deleted one (the server's FK does the same).
- **Visit prep** (B-58; `lib/appointments/visitPrep.ts`, pure; `components/visitPrep/`): `visitWindow` starts at the last past, not cancelled event linked to the providers, else 30 days back; `visitPrepSection` lists medication changes (pass retimes included) and the current list, each vital's range, average and direction against the same length before, symptoms with counts, highest severity and "new", events (not cancelled, not the last visit or this one) and overlapping care-period stays; empty sections are left out ("Nothing was logged in this time." when all are). A "Missed doses" section follows Current medications ("Lisinopril 10 mg: 1 missed, 1 refused (of 3)"), from `doseSlotsBetween` and `missedDoseCounts` over the window's doses. Entry points (writers): "Prepare for this visit" on an upcoming, live event's page; "Prepare for a visit" on a provider's page (their next appointment, or a confirm to create one through `appUrls.newAppointmentWith`, which saves once its day is picked and links the provider); Prepare in Upcoming rows' ⋯. Each opens `VisitPrepSheet` (start editable, at most now; preview; Insert). `entryActionItems` order: edit, prepare, reschedule, cancel/uncancel, print, delete. An event's page lists its open linked tasks (`LinkedTasks`, "Add task" prefilled with `eventId`); deleting the event clears their link (`taskService.releaseEvent`; see [tasks](tasks.md)).
- **Seeds** (`seeds/appointments.ts`, own ids, placed by `daysAhead`): about eight upcoming items for Dev Family (appointments with providers and places, one with an end, one all-day, a reminder, one cancelled, a rescheduled pair) and two past cancelled events; one for Dev Family 2.
- **Tests:** no area check (`upcoming.mjs` was moved, B-119).
