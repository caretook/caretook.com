# Appointments

The appointments area's rules and decisions. An orchestrator folds a landing's lasting decisions for this area in here.

PRD: [`plans/appointments-prd.md`](../../plans/appointments-prd.md); repeating ones: [`appointments-2`](../../plans/appointments-2-prd.md). **No new kind:** a future event is an appointment, and "upcoming" is worked out, never stored. **No reminders** (`planner-2`, B-248): later things are [todos](todos.md).

- **Columns on `timeline_entry`** (migration `0015_appointments`): `ends_at` (events; after `occurred_at`; null when all-day), `all_day` (notes and events; `occurred_at` is the start of the day in `time_zone`), `place` (events), `cancelled_at`, `cancelled_by` (set by the server, audit-style), `cancel_reason` (events), and `rescheduled_to` (events, only when cancelled): a composite FK with `ON DELETE SET NULL ("rescheduled_to")`, **hand-edited** (Drizzle can't express it); keep when regenerating. Other kinds refuse these fields (`db/schema/core/entryTiming.ts`); a `rescheduled_to` in another group is `timelineEntryService:rescheduledToNotInGroup`. Synced (`all_day` as 1/0).
- **Future dates:** only events may be ahead (`FUTURE_KINDS`, `FUTURE_KINDS_SQL`); other kinds are refused past now + 5 min on the device, now + 1 h on the server ("Can't be in the future"), notes only on create or a moved time (a stored future note stays editable, hidden until its time). Time fields max at now; `?when=pick` and `newPlannerEntry` are events only.
- **Time rules** (pure, tested, one home): `lib/appointments/upcoming.ts` (`entryTiming`: past, upcoming or now; all-day lasts to the end of its day in its zone; `upcomingOrder` soonest first, all-day first in a day; `upcomingOrderIn(zone)`/`listedDay` for lists grouped by the viewer's day; `nextUp` skips cancelled; `historyCutoff`), `upcomingDays.ts` (`upcomingSections`, "Now · until …" first; `pastSections`), `upcomingLabels.ts` (`timeRange`, `nowLabel`, `cancelledLabel`, `rescheduledLabel`, `nextStripText`). The history, upcoming (90 days) and past windows share `entryWindow`'s filter conditions.
- **The entry page:** the time region shows the range or "All day"; writers' time chip edits the end time (events) and all-day through `components/schedule`'s `ScheduleFields` (rules in `lib/appointments/entrySchedule.ts`). Events get a place row (`lib/appointments/entryPlace.ts`: empty shows the first linked provider's address, greyed "from <provider>"; either has a map action). `entries/new?kind=…&when=pick` (`WHEN_PARAM`, `WHEN_PICK`) opens the picker first, at 9:00 once a day is picked.
- **The Planner** (`routes/app/groups/planner/`, `appUrls.planner`, `/planner`; "Planner · Caretook"; the section after Medications, see [app-shell](app-shell.md)): live, by day soonest first; rows reuse `EntryBody`; call, map (`upcomingContact.ts`) and author sit under the time in `[data-when-column]`. `PlannerHeader`: `PageHeader` "Planner", for writers a + Menu "Add" (Event via `appUrls.newPlannerEntry`; Todo, whose sheet closes back to its tab; a title-less item reads "Event"), and `TabNav` Upcoming | Past | [Todos](todos.md), each carrying its filters. On Upcoming a `FilterMenu` beside the tabs, "Show": All, Events (`?kind=event`), Todos (`?show=todos`; `upcomingFilters`), with `FilterMenuChips` and an uncounted "By <name>" author chip. `planner/past` (`plannerPast`) lists past events newest first, cancelled included, paged; no Filter button.
- **Todos in the Planner** (`lib/todos/plannerTodos.ts`: `PlannerTodo`, `plannerTodos`, `withDueTodos`, `showsTodos`): open todos with a due day in the 90-day window are merged under their day in time order (overdue ones under Today), shown by `PlannerTodoRow` with a `TodoTick` (and `TodoUndoStatus`) under All and Todos (`?show=todos`), hidden under Events or an author filter; the recipient zone is read live. Never written as entries. The Planner shows no dose or pass rows (the Today card and Medications' Schedule tab keep theirs).
- **Cancel** (events, writers, past ones too): Cancel or Undo cancel before Delete in `entryActionItems`, a `CancelEventDialog` with an optional reason, a local write (`cancelEvent`, `uncancelEvent` in `lib/appointments/appointments.sync.ts`; `lib/appointments/eventCancel.ts`). Cancelled events are struck through and labelled everywhere, never "next", and the entry page shows a banner (reason, by whom, when; Undo for writers).
- **Reschedule** (`RescheduleSheet`, opened in place from the ⋯ menu on the timeline, the Planner and the entry page; no URL): "Keep the old one as cancelled" is on by default for an upcoming event (`lib/appointments/eventReschedule.ts`). Off is an ordinary time update. On is **one local transaction** (`rescheduleEvent`): the new event, its provider links, its document seeded by one `note_update` with the old document's full Yjs state, and the old event cancelled with `rescheduled_to`; then the new event opens. The old event's page shows "Rescheduled to <day>", notes read-only. `removeEntry` clears `rescheduled_to` on events pointing at the deleted one (the server's FK does the same).
- **Visit prep** (B-58; `visitPrep.ts`, pure; `components/visitPrep/`): `visitWindow` starts at the last past, not cancelled event linked to the providers, else 30 days back; `visitPrepSection` lists medication changes (pass retimes included) and the current list, each vital's range, average and direction against the same length before, symptoms with counts, highest severity and "new", events (not cancelled, not the last visit or this one) and overlapping care-period stays; empty sections are left out. "Missed doses" follows Current medications (`missedDoseCounts`). Entry points (writers): "Prepare for this visit" on an upcoming event's page; "Prepare for a visit" on a provider's page (their next appointment, or `appUrls.newAppointmentWith`, which links them); Prepare in Planner rows' ⋯. Each opens `VisitPrepSheet` (start editable, at most now; preview; Insert). `entryActionItems` order: edit, prepare, reschedule, cancel/uncancel, print, delete. An event's page lists its open linked todos (`LinkedTodos`, "Add todo" prefilled with `eventId`); deleting the event clears their link (`todoService.releaseEvent`; see [todos](todos.md)).
- **Repeating** (B-60; `0029_appointments_calendars`): `appointment_series` (synced; writers; `kind`: Zod takes `event` only, `topUp` skips stored note series; `rule` RRULE text with DAILY, WEEKLY or MONTHLY, INTERVAL, BYDAY (an ordinal for monthly, kept when edited), BYMONTHDAY, COUNT or UNTIL; `starts_on`, `start_time` (null all-day), `time_zone`, the template `title`, `duration_minutes`, `place`, `provider_ids`; `topped_up_through`; soft delete). `timeline_entry` gains `series_id` (composite FK, `ON DELETE SET NULL`, **hand-edited** like `rescheduled_to`, plus the `ALTER PUBLICATION`: keep both), `series_day` and `series_detached`, unique `(series_id, series_day)` among live rows. A **visit is an ordinary entry** with id UUID v5 of (series, day) (`seriesVisitId`), so the device and server converge; the first keeps the draft's id and notes. `seriesVisits` (rrule 2.8.1, floating wall-clock time placed by `zonedInstant`) lives only in `@caretook/data/series` (with `seriesEdit`), never the schema index: `budget.json` keeps rrule out of first load. `repeatText` says a rule in words; `lib/appointments/series.sync.ts` `createSeries` writes the series and 90 days of visits in one local transaction. **The server writes visits only for a series without `toppedUpThrough`**, and `appointmentSeriesService.topUp` (the calendar cron's last step) keeps every live series 90 days ahead from max(`topped_up_through` + 1, today in its zone), skipping days by `(series_id, series_day)`, passing over a failing one.
- **Editing a series** (`planSeriesEdit`, pure, `seriesEdit.ts`; `applySeriesEdit` on the device, one transaction): scope **only this visit** changes it and detaches it (cancel and reschedule detach too; later series edits leave it); **this and later** splits: the old series ends the day before (UNTIL, or is deleted when it is the first visit), a new one starts on this day, and later visits move onto it in place (same ids, notes, todos) when still in the rule, else are deleted unless they have notes, todos or a cancel (then detached). It takes time of day and length only (a day change goes through the rule). Past visits (`isPastVisit` on both sides: all-day lasts its day) are never written; "this and later" isn't offered on a started visit (`timelineEntryService:pastVisit`, 409). A split uploads the new series, the old PATCH, entry changes, then new visits. UI: `SeriesScopeDialog` (a `ChoiceDialog`; says "repeating event": `scopeQuestion(action)`) and `useSeriesScope` (`ONLY_THIS_VISIT_STATE`), an `editSeries` item in `entryActionItems`, `SeriesEditSheet`, `RepeatField` and `RepeatSheet` (`components/repeat/`), `RepeatMark` on rows and the Today card; the entry page asks the scope on title, time and place. Calendars: [calendars](calendars.md).
- **Seeds** (`seeds/appointments.ts`, own ids, placed by `daysAhead`): about eight upcoming items for Dev Family (providers and places, one all-day, one cancelled, a rescheduled pair; no future note or note series), two past cancelled events; one for Dev Family 2.
- **Tests:** Component tests on a fixed clock (Wed 2026-11-18 10:00 LA; `fixClock()` in `planner/plannerWorld.tsx`): the Planner, its tabs, Upcoming | Past, the Filter menu and no due doses or passes (`planner.browser.test.tsx`), its todos (`plannerTodos`), cancel, Undo, reschedule and its one local transaction (`plannerEvents`), and the timeline's Next strip and block (`timelineStrips`). `roundtrip` 6a keeps reschedule end to end (the new event, its link, document and notes, the old one cancelled pointing at it, on the other device and the server) and covers a series and its split. Series: `planner/plannerSeries.browser.test.tsx`; "Remind me": `planner/plannerRemind.browser.test.tsx`.
