## Problem

A care recipient's day is often in the hands of people who aren't in Caretook: a respite sitter, a neighbour, an agency aide, a relative visiting for a weekend. They need the facts on paper: who the person is, what they're allergic to, what they take and when, who to call, and what needs doing. Caretook prints three separate sheets from three different screens today, and nothing for providers, contacts or todos. A caretaker who wants a binder by the bed prints each one by hand, and after one change (a new medicine) can't easily reprint just the pages that changed.

## Solution

One **care booklet** per group: a printable page that lays out every booklet page in order, each on its own sheet of paper. It opens with the recipient's profile as the front page (allergies and conditions up front), then the medication list, the weekly bedside sheet, the emergency sheet, a providers list, a care team contacts sheet, and a hand-fillable todo sheet. A checklist above it picks which pages to print: print it all for a new binder, or untick all but the medication list after a dose change. Each page says what it is, whose it is and when it was printed. It reads the device's own copy, so it opens and prints offline, and each member sees only the pages their role can read. Later topics add the schedule, the write-in sheets that a member's AI reads back from a photo, the routine sheet, a cover page and guide pages, each as one more page file.

## User stories

1. As a caretaker, I want one booklet with all the recipient's printed sheets, so that I can make a bedside binder in one print.
2. As a caretaker, I want the booklet to open with the recipient's profile, allergies and conditions, so that anyone picking it up sees the critical facts first.
3. As a caretaker, I want to tick only the pages I need, so that after a change I reprint just those pages.
4. As a caretaker, I want each page to start on a new sheet of paper and name the page, the recipient and the date printed, so that a loose page is never mistaken for a current one.
5. As a member, I want the booklet's medication list, weekly sheet and emergency sheet to be the same sheets I print today, so that nothing I rely on changes.
6. As a viewer, I want to print the pages I can read, without the ones holding the recipient's private details, so that I can help without seeing more than my role allows.
7. As a member, I want to reach the booklet from the recipient's profile, so that printing their papers has one obvious place.
8. As a sitter, I want a providers list with each provider's specialty, phones and address, so that I can reach the right office.
9. As a sitter, I want a care team contacts sheet saying who to call first, the family's numbers, after-hours lines and the pharmacy, so that I know who to call for what, day or night.
10. As a sitter, I want a printed todo sheet I can tick off and write new todos on, so that I can keep track of the work without the app, and a member's AI can later bring my marks back.
11. As a caretaker, I want the booklet to work offline, so that I can print it from wherever I am.

## Implementation decisions

### The booklet area

- **A new area, `booklet`** (route folder `routes/app/groups/booklet/`, its `routes.ts` piece, `lib/booklet/urls.ts` with `bookletUrls.booklet(groupId, pages?)` composed into `appUrls`, a `check-areas.d/booklet` rule). The area doc `docs/domain/booklet.md` and its _Area index_ entry are written by the orchestrator at landing.
- **The page contract** (`BookletPage<T>`), one descriptor per page file in the booklet's `pages/` folder:
  - `id` (a `BookletPageId` string, also the `?pages=` token), `order` (a number), `title` (the page's name in the checklist and its header), `purpose` (one line: what the page is for), `marks` (how to mark it by hand, or null for a read-only page), `orientation` (`portrait | landscape`).
  - `visible(role)`: whether a member of that role gets the page (and so has the data on their device).
  - `load(db, groupId, now)`: the starting rows, read from the local database in the route's `clientLoader`, only for visible pages.
  - `Sheet`: a component taking `{ groupId, initial, now }` that keeps itself live (local live queries seeded with `initial`, as the existing sheets do) and renders the page.
- **Discovery:** the booklet module reads every page with `import.meta.glob(["./pages/*.tsx", "!./pages/*.test.tsx"], { eager: true })`, takes each module's `page` export, sorts by `order`, and fails loudly (a thrown error in dev and tests) on a duplicate id or order. Page files hold no tests; the booklet's tests sit beside the route.
- **Order numbers:** front page 10, medication list 20, weekly sheet 30, emergency sheet 40, providers list 50, contacts 60, todos 70. Reserved for `care-booklet-3`: cover 0, routine 15, schedule 75, Timeline 80, as-needed log 90, visit notes 100, medication changes 110. `care-booklet-2`'s guide pages take numbers after 20's neighbours as its planner picks.
- **Selection** (pure, in `lib/booklet/`, with its own unit test): `bookletSelection(pages, role, pagesParam)` returns the visible pages with a `selected` flag; no `?pages=` means all; unknown ids are ignored; an empty but present `?pages=` selects none. Ticking writes `?pages=` with `replace` (no history step per tick).
- **The route** (`…/booklet`, a `clientLoader` only, shared by web and native): the group's role, the visible pages' `load`s in parallel, `now` fixed when the page opens. On screen: a `BackLink` to the profile, the checklist (`CheckField` per visible page, in order, with its title and purpose), Print (`window.print()`, disabled with nothing ticked), then the ticked pages. In print only the ticked pages show, each in a `data-booklet-page="<id>"` wrapper that breaks before (`print:break-before-page`, none before the first); a landscape page's wrapper carries the weekly sheet's named page (`[page:weekly]`). Live queries keep it current; `shouldRevalidate` as on the existing print routes.
- **A shared page header** (`BookletPageHeader`, in the booklet folder): the page's title, the recipient's display name, the group and the printed date (`formatDate` in the recipient's zone). New pages use it; the existing sheets keep their own headers, which already carry these.

### The pages

- **Front page** (`front`): from the local recipient row (`recipientQuery`/profile sync), allergies (`loadAllergies`/`useAllergies`) and conditions (`loadConditions`/`useConditions`), rendered with the existing `PrintAllergies` and `PrintConditions` blocks; fields left out when empty (the note too); every role.
- **Medication list** (`medications`), **weekly sheet** (`weekly`, landscape, the week starting this Monday in the recipient's zone, the weekly route's own default) and **emergency sheet** (`emergency`, `visible` = `canReadRecipient(role, "details")`): thin page files over the existing loaders, hooks and sheet components, imported through a new `index.ts` in each of their folders. The standalone routes stay as they are.
- **Providers list** (`providers`): a pure builder over the local providers (live only) grouped by type in the directory's order (`PROVIDER_TYPES` order), then name; each row: name, specialty, phones with labels, address on one line; "No providers yet." when empty; every role.
- **Care team contacts** (`contacts`): a pure builder with sections Call first (`emergencyEntries` over the members and, for members who pass `canReadRecipient(role, "outsideContacts")`, outside contacts; primary marked), Care team (members with relationship and shared phones, from the member-share rows), Providers to call (live clinicians and practices: specialty, phones; a phone whose label matches /after.?hours/i is listed under an "After hours" sub-heading too), Pharmacies (live pharmacies with phones). Empty sections are left out; every role, with outside contacts only where the device has them.
- **Todos** (`todos`): a pure builder over the local open todos (not done, not deleted): due date first (overdue and today first, then by date, no due date last), then title; each row a tick box, title, due, assignee's name, an initials box; then `TODO_WRITE_IN_ROWS` (8) blank rows; `marks`: "Tick the box when it's done and add your initials. Write new todos on the blank lines." Every role.

## Testing decisions

- **Component tests** (vitest browser, [components](../docs/domain/components.md#component-tests)) for the route and each page, with the `fx` builders and `commands.media("print")` for the print layout: the checklist and selection (role filtering, `?pages=`, nothing ticked), the page breaks, each sheet's content and empty state. Prior art: `profile/print/emergencySheetPage.browser.test.tsx` and `medications/print/print.browser.test.tsx`.
- **The page contract and selection** (`lib/booklet/`) get a web:unit test (sorting, duplicate ids and orders, `?pages=`, role filtering).
- **Pure builders** (providers, contacts, todos) are tested through their page's component test, one test file per slice; a builder with many rules may add a web:unit test instead when its slice names it.
- No integration or end-to-end tests: no server, sync or schema change. The existing print tests stay green unchanged.

## Out of scope

- The schedule sheet, the write-in sheets (Timeline, as-needed dose log, visit notes, medication changes), the routine and preferences sheet and its new profile fields, and the cover page: `care-booklet-3`.
- Medication guide pages and the connectors that read photos of the todo and write-in sheets: `care-booklet-2` (held).
- Tracking what was printed or which pages changed since.
- Moving the existing sheets into a shared folder (a follow-up once `med-intervals` lands).
- PDF generation on the server; printing stays the browser's (Print or Save as PDF).

## Notes

- **The import exception:** the booklet's page files import the medication list, weekly sheet and emergency sheet through new `index.ts` files in `medications/print/`, `medications/print/week/` and `profile/print/`, against the components rule that a route never imports another route's folder. The reason: `med-intervals` owns `routes/app/groups/medications/` and edits the weekly sheet. At landing the orchestrator files a `fixes-nit` item to move the three sheets into a feature folder once `med-intervals` has landed.
- This topic runs after `data-rights` (it owns `routes/app/groups/profile/`, whose header this topic changes); it reads `lib/` modules other topics own without editing them.
- `care-booklet-3`'s planner: the cover page renders from the descriptors' `title`, `purpose` and `marks`, so each write-in page brings its own instructions; the as-needed log's minimum gap has no field today (as-needed holds only `maxPerDay`), so that page needs either a new schedule field or `med-intervals`' intervals; the routine sheet needs new profile fields (a migration).
