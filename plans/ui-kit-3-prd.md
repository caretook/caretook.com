**The skeptic pass** (one fresh agent, before landing) found nothing that needed asking. It changed:

- the section map is built in slice 2, so slices 3 and 4 only read it and no longer depend on each other;
- the field fill leaves ghost inputs alone, restates the disabled look and repaints placeholders (daisyUI's are 50% of the text colour, under 4.5:1);
- dialogs already animate through daisyUI, so slice 5 re-times them instead of adding motion;
- `Sheet`'s hold is narrowed: the topmost open sheet only (the router takes one blocker, and sheets stack), a change of path only (a filter sheet's chips and a form's own submission pass through), with tests for each; a save is described as it is (the caller navigates after its action);
- `quick-entry-2` is named in the start conditions (it shares quick entry's cards and reads the entry metadata slice 2 reshapes), slices 2 and 4 read their feature docs, slice 6 says it appends motion rules, and the PRD table's hot files are this topic's, with the guard's moving to `ui-kit-4`.

Declined: its count of `Sheet` call sites (33); a search finds 41 in 38 files, and the decision doesn't turn on the number.

## Problem

The dark theme is close to black: the page, the cards on it and the lines between them sit so near each other that screens feel heavy and flat. In both themes a text box is painted the same colour as the card, sheet or band it sits on, so it's hard to see where to type.

Colour carries little meaning. The timeline's entry icons borrow the status colours: a symptom is the warning amber, an event the info blue, so a caretaker can't tell "this is a symptom" from "this needs attention", and the Today card's real urgency colours compete with them. Every section looks the same at the top: the same title, the same primary-tinted tab, the same grey "No … yet." line.

Menus, popovers and toasts appear and vanish in one frame, dialogs move on daisyUI's own slower timing, and a sheet slides in but disappears at once when closed, which reads as a glitch next to the motion the rest of the kit now has.

## Solution

Dark mode is lifted to a soft indigo-grey with clear steps between page, card and border. Text boxes get their own fill in both themes. A small palette of accent colours, kept apart from the status colours, gives each section and each kind of timeline entry its own hue: a chip beside the page title, the current tab, the empty state's icon and the entry icons all agree. Status colours go back to meaning only status. Overlays fade in and out, and a sheet leaves the way it came. People who ask for reduced motion see no movement, and Easy mode stays plain.

The rest of the app moves onto the kit in `ui-kit-4`, which ends with the guard.

## User stories

1. As a caretaker using dark mode, I want the page, cards and borders to be lighter and clearly apart, so that screens don't feel like a black slab.
2. As a caretaker, I want secondary text, links, errors and badges to stay readable on the lifted dark surfaces, so that the lighter theme costs no legibility.
3. As a caretaker in either theme, I want a text box to look different from what it sits on, so that I can see where to type.
4. As a caretaker, I want a disabled text box to still look disabled, so that the new fill doesn't mislead me.
5. As a caretaker, I want printed sheets to stay black on white, so that the colour work doesn't change paper.
6. As a caretaker, I want each kind of timeline entry to have its own colour that is not a warning, error, info or success colour, so that colour tells me the kind and never implies urgency.
7. As a caretaker, I want a dose and a medication change to share the Medications colour, and an event the Planner's, so that the timeline and the sections agree.
8. As a caretaker who can't tell some colours apart, I want every entry to keep its icon and label, so that colour is never the only signal.
9. As a caretaker, I want each section to show its colour as a small icon chip beside the page title, so that I know where I am at a glance.
10. As a caretaker, I want the current tab on Medications and the Planner to carry the section's colour, so that the tabs belong to the page.
11. As a caretaker, I want an empty section to show its icon in the section's colour above the "nothing yet" line, so that an empty page looks deliberate, not broken.
12. As a caretaker using Easy mode, or with an easy role, I want the pages to stay neutral, so that the simple view stays simple.
13. As a caretaker, I want every accent's text and icon to be readable in light and dark, so that colour never costs contrast.
14. As a caretaker, I want menus and popovers to fade in and out, so that they don't pop.
15. As a caretaker, I want confirm and choice dialogs, and the dimming behind them, to fade in and out, so that a question arrives gently.
16. As a caretaker, I want the update notice and other toasts to fade in and out, so that they don't flash.
17. As a caretaker, I want a sheet to close with the reverse of its opening, however I close it, so that closing feels like opening.
18. As a caretaker who has asked for reduced motion, I want none of this to move or delay anything, so that the app respects my setting.
19. As a keyboard or screen reader user, I want focus to return to where it was after any overlay closes, exactly as today, so that motion costs no accessibility.
20. As a developer, I want the accents, the icon chip and the field fill in the kit and its gallery in both themes, so that the next screen uses them instead of inventing colours.

## Implementation decisions

**Surfaces and the field fill (B-520, B-481)**

- The dark theme's three base tokens change in the theme block; light's don't. The values in the _Decisions_ table are the starting point; the contrast tests decide the final ones within B-520's bounds (page about 23%, cards about 30%, chroma at most 0.025).
- One new token, the field fill, with a light and a dark value, applied site-wide to daisyUI's `input`, `select` and `textarea` (so hand-written controls outside the kit get it too) and to print as white. Ghost inputs stay transparent, the disabled look is restated over it, and placeholders take a colour that keeps 4.5:1. `Field` needs no new prop. The notes editor and quick entry's box are not text boxes in this sense and keep their surfaces.
- What depends on the bases is rechecked and fixed where it fails: `text-muted` and `text-faint` (4.5:1 on the page, a card and a field), the tab bar's dark surface, the timeline's sticky day band, `TabNav`'s bar and current tab, the search `Highlight`, and the kit's coloured text (`LINK`, the field error and warning lines, `Message`, `Badge`). A failing text colour is lifted in the kit's shared class or constant; status tokens and fills stay daisyUI's.

**Accents (B-521)**

- A deep, pure module in the kit: the accent names and one function from an accent (or neutral) and a look (`solid`, `soft`, `text`) to classes. Behind it, three CSS variables per accent per theme at the end of `app.css`. Nothing outside it names an accent colour.
- `IconChip` (`icon`, `accent?`, `look` solid or soft, `size`, `shape` round or rounded; decorative, hidden from the accessibility tree unless given a label): in `ui` and `ui/plain`.
- Two pure maps beside their owners: entry type to accent and look (the timeline's display metadata, replacing its class strings), and section to accent and icon (the app shell; a map of its own, since Care periods is not in the section registry).

**Using them (B-522, B-523, B-524)**

- The entry-type icon and quick entry's cards render `IconChip` from the entry map. The Today card's urgency tints are untouched.
- `PageHeader` takes `icon` and `accent` (a soft chip before the title, decorative). `TabNav` takes `accent` (default: today's primary look). The five accented sections' pages pass them; a page in Easy mode or for an easy role passes none.
- `EmptyState` takes `icon` and `accent`: a soft circle above the line; without them it is today's quiet line. The accented sections' main lists adopt it.

**Overlay motion (B-471)**

- Fades use the kit's motion variables, sit under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, and are owned by the primitives: `Menu`, `Popover`, the native dialogs (daisyUI's modal transition re-timed in one rule for every modal dialog, so hand-written ones get it), `Toast` (`open`, mounted through its exit; its two callers pass `open`).
- A menu's exit stays shorter than the wait `useShowModal` gives a closing menu, so a dialog opened from a menu item still returns focus to the menu's button.
- `Sheet` plays its exit before its caller hears of a dismissal, and holds a navigation away from an open route sheet for the exit's length: the topmost open sheet only, a change of path only, never a submission. Under reduced motion nothing is held. Focus return is unchanged.

**Unchanged:** every URL, loader, permission, schema, sync, the server, emails, print layouts, the light theme's surfaces, daisyUI's status tokens. No migration, no dependency.

## Testing decisions

- Good tests here measure what a person sees (a computed contrast ratio, a role and name, whether a closed overlay is gone) and never a class name's spelling. Prior art: the kit's token and `TabNav` contrast tests (`apps/web/test/contrast.ts`), `motion.browser.test.tsx` (reduced motion emulated from the test file), the gallery test, the pure `buttonClass` and `badgeClass` unit tests.
- **Unit:** the accent module (every accent and look gives classes; none names a status, primary or secondary token); the entry-type and section maps (every entry type and every accented section has an entry; Easy gives none).
- **Component:** contrast in both themes for the lifted surfaces, the field fill, the kit's coloured text, every accent (text on its tint and on both surfaces at 4.5:1; an icon on its solid and soft chip at 3:1); `IconChip`, `PageHeader`, `TabNav`, `EmptyState` with and without an accent; one screen test per adopter (a timeline row per entry type, a section page's header and empty list, Easy mode neutral); overlays open, close and return focus with motion on and with reduced motion; a sheet's exit on each way of closing.
- **Browser checks:** none new. The `ui` check and the checks that open and close sheets (`roundtrip`) run at the gates; the sheet-exit slice has a stack and runs the changed checks itself.

## Out of scope

- The area sweeps, the profile renames and the guard (`ui-kit-4`).
- A tinted Today card and a standalone header wash (cut in the colour plan).
- Accents on detail pages, the phone tab bar, the header's section links, `/admin` and marketing.
- Tooltip motion, page transitions, and a sheet's exit after its route is replaced by another sheet.
- Changing daisyUI's status, primary or secondary tokens, or the light theme's surfaces.
