## Problem

Quick entry is the fastest way to log care, and a caretaker trusts the card it shows. The review found lines where the card is confidently wrong:

- "Did not give metoprolol" is logged as a **given** dose, as are "metoprolol not given" and "couldn't give…". The medication record then says a dose was given that wasn't.
- "Gave 3 tylenol" records the usual "2 tabs" and leaves the 3 in the note; "gave half a tylenol" too. "Tylenol 2 at 8" becomes a symptom named Tylenol, severity 2.
- Times shift without a word: "sat up in chair" is dated last Saturday, "2nd tylenol" Nov 2, "11/25 at 2pm" tomorrow, "dr alvarez at 2" 2 AM, "pain 8 a bit better" 8:00 AM, and "this morning" typed before 8:00 is refused as being in the future.
- The box can get stuck: remove a card, delete the text, type the same line again, and there's no card, no Save and Enter does nothing.

## Solution

The same box, cards and Save; the reading is right:

- A line that says a dose wasn't given shows a **Skipped** card (or a note), never Given, and never gives a whole pass.
- A count typed beside a medication is the amount on the card and in the record.
- Words that aren't times ("sat up", "2nd tylenol", "8 a bit") leave the time alone; "11/25" is a date; a planned "at 2" is the afternoon; "this morning" works first thing in the morning.
- Removing a card never outlives the text it belonged to, and Clear is there whenever there's text.

## Implementation decisions

- **The parser** (`parseQuickEntry`, pure, client-safe) keeps its interface: text and context in, parts with ranked readings out. No action shape changes, so the device runner, the server runner and the connector tools are untouched.
- **Negation** is one helper both the dose reading and the pass reading use. Negating words: not, never, no, the n't forms with or without the apostrophe (didn't, don't, couldn't, can't, wasn't, hasn't, haven't), unable, forgot. **Position decides for a dose:** a negating word within three words before a give or take verb, "not given" / "wasn't given" after the name, or a negating word opening a part that carries the verb over ("…and not metoprolol", "no metoprolol") makes the status `skipped`; a negating word after the verb is ordinary note text. Did not / would not before take, have or swallow stays `refused`. Before a skip or refuse verb the part has no dose reading. The pass reading returns nothing on a negating word anywhere in the part. A negated dose's confidence sits below a plain one and above the note. Not covered: two medications in one part ("gave metformin but not lisinopril" reads metformin given, as now).
- **Counts:** the amount finder also takes a count with no unit when it sits directly before the medication's matched words (with "of" or "a" between allowed) or directly after them ("tylenol 2", "tylenol x2"). Counts are digits, decimals, fractions, ½, half, quarter, one to four. The recorded amount is the count with the usual dose's unit word when the usual dose is a count and a unit, singular for one or less and plural above, else the count's own words. After the name, a number equal to the strength number isn't a count; anywhere, a number above 20 isn't. A medication match of any strength (its name, or a brand word in its directions) with a count is a dose line without a verb, at the name-only confidence.
- **No symptom named after a medication:** the symptom reading is dropped when its name is made of words the same part matched to a medication (by name or by directions).
- **Finding times** (`findTime`): the ambiguous short weekdays, ordinals, the separate "a"/"p" and month/day dates follow the _Decisions_ rows. A month/day is checked for a real month and day; the day spec is the existing month-and-date one (plus a year when given).
- **Resolving times** (`resolveTime` and the parser's time state): reading forward with no day and no am/pm, candidates before 7:00 are skipped. A clock beside a morning period is am. A period with no clock, reading back, today or with no day said: when the period has begun (morning from 4:00, afternoon from 12:00, evening from 17:00, night from 20:00) and its usual hour is still ahead, the past-only readings take now. The notes-ahead test (`noteAhead`) keeps seeing the period's usual hour, so a plain note line still offers the todo first; its note card is dated now, as today.
- **The box:** card state (chosen reading, removed, per-card error) is kept only for keys in the current parts; the pruning is a pure function beside the card rules. Clear shows when the box has text.
- **Docs:** the orchestrator folds the new rules into the assistant area doc at landing; loops don't edit it.

## Testing decisions

- The parser and the time rules are pure: unit tests, table-driven, a row for every case named in the items and for each neighbour that must keep working ("o2 sat 95", "sat 2pm", "Dr. Lee the 2nd", "the 2nd dose", "gave half a tablet metoprolol", "wouldn't take metoprolol", "gave evening meds", "bp 120/80"). Prior art: `quickEntry.test.ts`. The time slices put their rows in a new `quickEntryTime.test.ts` so they don't collide with the dose slices.
- The stuck box: a unit test of the pruning function (prior art: `quickEntryCards.test.ts`) and one component test of remove, delete, retype in the timeline's quick entry test (`timelineQuickEntry.browser.test.tsx`).
- No integration test and no browser check: nothing crosses the server or sync.

## Out of scope

- Skipping or refusing a whole pass in one line.
- Day-first dates, other languages, a year alone.
- A warning for an amount that differs from the usual dose.
- Any change to the actions, the runners, the connector tools or stored entries. Entries already saved with a wrong reading stay as they are; the user edits them.
- The parser's move to `@caretook/data/domain` (`code-health`).
