## Problem

A caretaker who has connected their own Claude or ChatGPT to a care group can ask it what doses are due today, but not what tomorrow or the coming week looks like: "what does Mum take on Saturday morning?", "write me the week's medication rounds for the sitter". `upcoming` deliberately leaves doses out, and `todays_doses` stops at midnight.

The same caretaker can ask the assistant to add a todo, but not one that repeats: "remind us to put the bins out every Monday" makes a single todo, and they have to open the app to set the repeat.

Separately, Caretook isn't in Claude's or ChatGPT's directories, so connecting means pasting a URL. That stays so until after the launch.

## Solution

- A new read tool, **`dose_schedule`**, gives the medication plan for today onward: for each day (a week by default, two at most) the rounds in the recipient's time, each with its medications and amounts, and the as-needed medications with their daily maximum. It says nothing about what was given; `todays_doses` keeps that.
- **`add_todo` takes a `repeat`**: every day, every week on chosen days, or every month, optionally every N. The todo it makes repeats exactly as one made in the app's todo form: ticking it makes the next.
- The directory listings (B-196) move to their own held topic, with what a listing needs written down.

## User stories

## Implementation decisions

### The schedule ahead

- **A pure module, `doseSchedule`**, beside the due-dose core in the data package's schema (client-safe, though only the server uses it now): given the medications, the passes, a first day, a count of days and the time zone, it returns each day's rounds. A round is a pass, or a time of its own, with its time, the instant it falls at, and its medications by name with their amounts. It is built on the existing range reader (`doseSlotsBetween` with no doses, current medications only, up to the last day's end, the slots' states dropped), never a second copy of the slot rules and no edit to the due-dose core: one slot per medication per time, a pass ahead of a time of its own, DST-safe days, a medication only from the day it starts or was added. Each day's slots are grouped as `duePasses` groups today's: a pass's medications together, a medication's own time as a round of its own, so a day here has the same rounds as `todays_doses`. It also returns the as-needed medications with their maximum.
- **The tool `dose_schedule`** (read scope, `readOnlyHint`): input `from` (a day, default today in the recipient's zone) and `days` (1 to 14, default 7). A `from` before today is an `isError` result whose message points to `list_timeline` for doses already logged. It loads what `todays_doses` loads, minus the dose entries: current, live medications with a schedule (through `normalizeSchedule`) and the passes, as the member.
  - **Structured result:** the time zone, the first day, `days` (each with its day, weekday and rounds: name, time, instant in the recipient's offset, medications with id, title and amount) and `asNeeded` (id, title, maximum a day).
  - **Text:** a line per round; consecutive days with the same rounds are written once ("Mon Nov 23 to Sun Nov 29, each day:"). A day or range with nothing scheduled says so.
  - Its description says it is the plan, not what was given, and names `todays_doses` for today's state. `upcoming`'s description names both dose tools; `todays_doses`'s description gains one sentence pointing here (its handler isn't touched: `dose-accuracy` edits it).
- It joins the tools list after `todays_doses`. The server instructions and the prompts don't change.
- **Nothing new is returned:** medication ids, titles (name and strength), amounts, pass names and times, all of which `todays_doses` and `list_medications` send. The read tools' never-returned sweep covers the new tool.

### A repeating todo

- **`add_todo`'s input gains `repeat`** (optional): `every` (`day`, `week` or `month`), `interval` (1 to the series limit, default 1) and `weekdays` (two-letter days, only with `week`). The tool turns it into the rule text with the shared rule builder and passes it as the action's `repeatRule` (planner-3's field), so the todo service, the repeat columns and the next-occurrence rules are untouched. RRULE text is never an input: a raw `repeatRule` sent to the tool is still ignored.
- **A small pure helper** (in the tool's module) maps `repeat`, the due day and today to the rule and the first day:
  - `week` without `weekdays` repeats on the due day's weekday (today's, with no due day).
  - `month` repeats on the due day's day of the month; a 29th to 31st behaves as planner-3's quick entry and the todo form do in a short month.
  - No due day: the first day is today, or for a week with `weekdays` the next listed day, today included (the recipient's zone).
  - A due day given with `weekdays` that doesn't fall on one of them is refused (the tool's usual invalid result, path `repeat`), and the message names the next day that does.
  - `weekdays` with `day` or `month` is refused.
- **The answer** is "Added todo: …" as now, with the repeat in `todoRepeatText`'s words ("Every Mon"), and the structured result gains `repeats` (the same text `list_todos` gives) and the `dueOn` used.
- The description says how to make a repeating todo and that a member stops it in the app. `via` is set as for any todo the tool adds.

# Wave 2

## Wave 2 problem

A caretaker whose own assistant is connected can ask it about the providers and medications already in the app, but can't have it add one: "add Dr. Priya Shah, cardiology at Riverside Heart, 555-0142" or "we started metformin 500 mg, a prescription, morning and evening" end with "open the app". Typing a provider or a medication into a sheet on a phone is the slowest part of setting up a group.

Many families also keep a paper sheet by the bed: Caretook's printed weekly sheet, ticked G, S or R through the week by whoever gave the doses, often a sitter or relative without the app. Today someone has to copy each tick into the app by hand, slot by slot, and check which ones were already logged. The member's assistant can read a photo of the sheet, but it has no way to see last week's slots and their states, and `log_dose` writes one dose at a time with no word on what was already there.

## Wave 2 solution

- **`add_provider`** adds a clinician, practice, pharmacy or other contact to the group's directory with the Add provider sheet's fields and checks, and answers in quick entry's words with a link.
- **`add_medication`** adds a current medication with its type and schedule (as needed, or in med times or at times of its own), optionally its prescriber and pharmacy from the directory, through the same schemas and service as the Add medication sheet; the "started" entry on the timeline shows "via" the assistant.
- **`past_doses`** gives the dose record for past days: each day's rounds with every slot's state (given, skipped, refused, missed, or open for today) and who logged it, in the same shape as the printed sheet.
- **`log_past_doses`** takes the sheet's marks in one call and answers for each: added, already logged, or a conflict to ask the member about (the paper says refused, the app says given), writing only what isn't already recorded.

## Wave 2 user stories

18. As a caretaker using my own assistant, I want to tell it a new provider's name, specialty or practice, phone and address and have it added, so that I don't type it into a form.
19. As a caretaker, I want the assistant to add a clinician at a practice already in the directory, so that the provider's page shows where they work.
20. As a caretaker, I want the assistant to refuse a provider the Add provider sheet would refuse (no name, a bad phone number, a practice that isn't one), with the reason, so that it can ask me and try again.
21. As a caretaker, I want a provider already in the directory not to be added twice when the assistant repeats itself, so that the directory stays clean.
22. As a caretaker, I want to tell the assistant about a new medication with its type, strength, dose and when it's taken, and have it added as the Add medication sheet would, so that it appears on Today from its first dose.
23. As a caretaker, I want the assistant to put a medication in our med times by name ("Morning and Evening") or at a time of its own, so that it joins the rounds we already give.
24. As a caretaker, I want the assistant to name the prescriber and pharmacy from our directory, so that refills and visit prep find them.
25. As a caretaker, I want a medication the assistant adds to show "via" the assistant on its "started" entry, so that the family can see where it came from.
26. As a caretaker, I want the assistant to refuse a medication without a type or schedule, or with a med time we don't have, naming what's missing and our med times, so that it asks me rather than guessing.
27. As a caretaker, I want to photograph last week's paper sheet and ask my assistant to log it, so that a week of ticks is in the app in one step.
28. As a caretaker, I want the assistant to see which slots were already logged in the app and by whom, so that it only adds what's missing.
29. As a caretaker, I want to be told which slots disagree (the paper says refused, the app says given) without anything being changed, so that I settle them myself.
30. As a caretaker, I want marks the assistant misread (a medication not taken at that time, a day not on the sheet, a slot still ahead) reported back instead of logged, so that a bad reading never invents a dose.
31. As a caretaker, I want a sheet's doses logged at their scheduled times and amounts and grouped into each med time's round, so that the timeline reads like a week of Given all.
32. As a caretaker, I want logging the same sheet twice to add nothing the second time, so that a retry is safe.
33. As a viewer, I want to read past doses too, so that a read-only connection can answer "did Dad get his evening pills on Tuesday?".

## Wave 2 implementation decisions

### Adding a provider

- **Shared wording:** `newItemPreview` (the quick entry card's title and detail for a new medication or provider) moves from the web's quick entry cards into the data package's quick entry new-item module, beside the drafts it reads; the web module imports it from there and its behaviour and tests are unchanged.
- **The tool `add_provider`** (write scope, write annotations), in its own module in the tools folder:
  - Input: `type` (clinician, practice, pharmacy, other; default clinician), `name`, `specialty`, `practiceId` (an id from `list_providers`), `phones` (each a `number` and an optional `label`), `address`, `website`, each with the provider schema's limits. Unknown keys (email, primary, status) are dropped.
  - It checks for a duplicate first: a live provider in the group of the same type whose name folds (`foldSearchText`, spacing collapsed) to the same text answers "Already in the directory, nothing added: <name>" with its id and link, and writes nothing.
  - Otherwise `providerService.create` as the member with the group from the grant: its schema and practice checks are the sheet's. A refusal is the usual `isError` result with the issues' paths and messages.
  - **Answer:** "Added provider: <title> · <detail>" (`newItemPreview` on a draft made from the input, the practice named), the link to the provider's page, and the structured `providerId`, `preview`, `url`. No "via" line: providers don't store it.
  - Description: what it adds and takes, that ids come from `list_providers`, and to ask the member before calling.
- **Links:** `providerPath` (and, for the next tool, `medicationPath`) join the connector's links, equal to `appUrls`' provider and medication pages and tested beside the others.
- It joins the tools list at the end of the writes.

### Adding a medication

- **`medicationService.create` gains a server-only `via` option** (beside `changedAt`): the "started" change entry it writes carries it, as `timelineEntryService.create`'s does. The create schema still strips a `via` sent as input, and device uploads never pass one.
- **The tool `add_medication`** (write scope), in its own module:
  - Input: `name`, `type` (required, `medicationTypeZSchema`), `strength`, `form`, `dose`, `directions`, `reason`, `startedOn`, `prescriberId`, `pharmacyId` (ids from `list_providers`), and `schedule` (required): `{ type: "asNeeded", maxPerDay? }` or `{ type: "scheduled", slots: [{ medTime?: name, time?: "HH:MM", amount? }] }`, exactly one of `medTime` and `time` per slot.
  - A `medTime` is matched against the group's live med times by name, any case; an unknown one is an issue at `schedule.slots.<n>.medTime` whose message lists the group's med times with their times. Matched slots become `{ passId }`; the schedule then goes through `medicationScheduleZSchema` (1 to 8 distinct slots, as the sheet).
  - Duplicate check: a current, live medication whose name folds to the same text and whose strength folds to the same text (both empty counts as the same) answers "Already on the medication list, nothing added" with its id and link.
  - Otherwise `medicationService.create` as the member with `status: "current"` and `via` the client's name: its prescriber, pharmacy and pass checks and its "started" entry are the sheet's.
  - **Answer:** "Added medication: <title> · <detail>" (`newItemPreview`), then "Schedule: <scheduleText>", the link to its page, `Shown as "via <client>" on its started entry.`; structured `medicationId`, `preview`, `schedule` (the text), `url`, `via`.
  - Description: type and schedule are required and the assistant should ask for them; med times by name (as `todays_doses` and `dose_schedule` name them); prescriber and pharmacy ids from `list_providers`; ask before calling.

### The dose record (past slots)

- **The range reader gains an optional `now`:** `doseSlotsBetween` keeps every slot up to `to` but settles states at `now` when given (today being `now`'s day), so a range ending at today's end shows today's later slots as upcoming. Without `now` it behaves as it does now (settled at `to`); visit prep and `doseSchedule` don't pass it.
- **A pure module, `doseRecord`**, beside `doseSchedule` in the data package's medications schema and exported with it: given medications (current and stopped, not deleted), passes (deleted too), doses, schedule changes, a first day, a count of days, `now` and the zone, it returns each day's rounds (`duePasses`' grouping, as `doseSchedule` does) with each slot's state, amount and answering dose. Built on `doseSlotsBetween` with the changes, from the first day's start to the last day's end and settled at `now`: each day has the schedule it had then, a stopped medication counts up to its stop day, today's later slots are upcoming and earlier days' unanswered ones missed. Days after `now`'s day are never asked for.
- **The tool `past_doses`** (read scope), after `dose_schedule` in the list:
  - Input: `from` (a day, default 6 days before today in the recipient's zone), `days` (1 to 14, default 7). Days after today are left out; a `from` after today is an invalid result pointing to `dose_schedule`; a `from` more than 90 days back is refused.
  - Loads what `todays_doses` loads, over the range (doses from the day before `from`, changes from two days before, as the range reader wants).
  - **Structured:** `timeZone`, `from`, `to`, `days` (each `day`, `weekday`, `rounds`: `passId`, `name`, `time`, `slots`: `medicationId`, `medication`, `amount`, `time`, `at`, `state`, `dose` (`entryId`, `status`, `loggedAt`, `loggedBy`) or null).
  - **Text:** a day heading, a line per round, each slot's medication and state ("Given by Sam at 8:12", "Missed"); no rounds: "No scheduled doses".
  - Description: past doses with their states, for checking a week or a paper sheet against the app; the marks for `log_past_doses` name a slot by `medicationId`, `day` and `time` as listed here; today's live state is `todays_doses`'.
- Nothing new is returned: the same fields `todays_doses` sends, over more days.

### Logging a dose sheet

- **Marks:** `{ medicationId, day, time, status: given | skipped | refused, note? }`, at most 200 a call (`sheetDoseMarkZSchema`, client-safe, beside `doseRecord`).
- **A pure classifier, `sheetDoseOutcomes`** (in `doseRecord`'s module): given the record (settled at `now`, so a slot later today is listed as upcoming) for the marks' days, `now` and the marks, each mark's outcome: `add` (the slot is open: missed, or due or overdue today), `alreadyLogged` (its dose has the mark's status), `conflict` (its dose has another status; carries the recorded status and dose), `noSlot` (no slot for that medication, day and time: the medication isn't taken then, isn't in the group, or was deleted), `notYet` (the slot is after `now`), `tooOld` (before the 14-day reach), `repeated` (a later mark for a slot an earlier mark named). One source: no slot rule of its own.
- **`assistantActionService.logPastDoses(ctx, groupId, marks, { via, now })`**, the runner's sibling and sharing its private helpers: one transaction, the writer check and recipient as `run` does; the marks parsed (a malformed batch: `assistantActionService:invalid`, nothing written); medications, passes and changes loaded once for the marks' days (a day either side); each mark's slot id (`doseSlotIdFor`, from the slot the schedule gives) locked in a stable order before the doses are read, so two simultaneous batches add each dose once; then the doses read and the record built and classified; then each `add` written as a dose entry with `via`: occurred at the slot's moment, the slot's amount, `passId` and `scheduledFor` named, the slot's id (random if taken, as `scheduledDose` does), and one round per med time and day (`passLogId`: a round a dose there already has, else `passRoundId`). Returns each mark's outcome with the entry id for `added`, `alreadyLogged` and `conflict`.
- **The tool `log_past_doses`** (write scope), after `give_pass` in the list: input `marks`; it runs `logPastDoses` and answers with counts ("Added 18 doses, 4 already logged, 1 to check"), a line per conflict ("Tue Nov 24 Evening, Metformin 500 mg: the sheet says refused, the app says given (logged by Sam)") and per refused mark with its reason; structured `results` in the marks' order (`outcome`, the mark, `entryId`, `recorded` for a conflict), `added`, and `via`. Nothing added gives "Nothing added" with the reasons.
- Description: for logging a paper sheet or a past week at once; read `past_doses` first and name slots as it lists them; marks only for scheduled slots (as-needed doses: `log_dose` with their time); conflicts are not changed: ask the member, who fixes them in the app; ask before calling.
