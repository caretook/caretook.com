## Problem

A caretaker splitting "Get ready for the hospital stay" into steps has to open the todo, scroll to a Subtasks band, add the steps there, and open each step to add the next level. The Todos page shows the parent as "1 of 3 done" and the steps only when they have a date or an assignee, as separate rows marked "Part of …", so nobody sees the plan as a whole. Steps get dates one by one, and nothing stops a step being due after the thing it's a step of.

## Solution

The Todos page shows each todo with its subtasks indented under it, every level, in a sensible order. A + on any row adds a subtask right there: type a title, press Enter, type the next. A todo's own page shows the same tree from one level down, with the same + at each level and a field to add a child of this todo. A new subtask is due when its parent is, never later; moving the parent's date earlier brings its steps with it.

## User stories

1. As a caretaker, I want the Todos page to show each todo's subtasks indented under it, at every level, so that I see the whole plan.
2. As a caretaker, I want to see a done step struck through in its place, so that I see how far along the plan is.
3. As a caretaker filtering to Mine, a tag or some text, I want a step that matches to show under its parents, so that I know what it's part of.
4. As a caretaker, I want an overdue step to put its plan under Overdue, so that nothing late hides.
5. As a writer, I want a + on a row that opens a title field, adds a subtask on Enter and stays open for the next, so that I can lay out steps quickly.
6. As a writer, I want that + on a todo's own page at every level and a field to add a child of the todo, so that I add steps where I'm looking.
7. As a viewer, I don't see a + anywhere, so that I'm not offered what I can't do.
8. As a writer, I want no + on a todo at the deepest level or on a repeating todo, so that I'm not offered a step that can't be saved.
9. As a writer, I want a new subtask to be due when its parent is, so that I don't date each step by hand.
10. As a writer, I want the app to stop me setting a step's due day after its parent's, so that the plan stays possible.
11. As a writer moving a parent's date earlier, I want its open steps that would be late to move with it, so that the plan stays consistent.
12. As a caretaker, I want steps listed dated first by due, then undated by when they were added, so that what's next is on top.
13. As a caretaker using the Planner, I want each day to show a plan's tree, and a step due earlier than its plan on its own day under its dimmed parents, so that I see the plan and nothing early hides.
14. As a member who syncs to Google Calendar or subscribes to the feed, I want only top-level todos there, so that a plan's steps don't fill my calendar.
15. As a caretaker glancing at the Today card, I want it to name the step that's due, so that I know what to do now.
16. As an easy-view member, I want my steps shown under the todo they're part of, so that I know what each is for.

## Implementation decisions

- **Tree rules, pure and shared** (the tree rules module, both sides): `dueLimitOf(parent)` (the parent's day, or none), `clampDue(child, parent)` (a child's due capped at the parent's day, taking the parent's time when capped), `inheritedDue(parent)`, `siblingOrder` (dated by day and time, then undated, each then by created and id), `upcomingListsSubtask` (the connectors' `upcoming` only): a subtask lists when assigned or due before its parent's day; `listsOnItsOwn` goes once no list shows a subtask alone.
- **The service** (`todoService`): `create` (and `upsert`'s first write) inherit the parent's due when the input has none, and clamp; `update` clamps a subtask, and on a parent given any new day clamps its open live descendants, as the actor, in the same transaction, through the same write path as any update (so each queues its calendar push). Nothing new is refused, so an upload never fails on this rule.
- **Calendars** (the calendar items rules, `calendarFeedService`, `calendarSyncService`): the feed and the Google push leave subtasks out where they load todos (the calendar items rules are shared with reminders, which keep them); the push's existing plan then deletes any event it holds for a subtask. The connectors' `upcoming` uses `upcomingListsSubtask` and names the parent.
- **The device** (`todos.sync`): `createTodo` with a parent inherits; `updateTodo` refuses a change of a subtask's day to after its parent's with an issue the form shows (other edits of a late subtask clamp), and writes the descendants' clamps in the parent's transaction (the uploads are plain PATCHes the service clamps again). The tree lists share one query: the group's open todos and the trees' done children.
- **The tree view** (a pure lib module): from the todos and the filter, the trees in list order, each placed by its most urgent open todo, siblings by `siblingOrder`, matches with their ancestors kept; for the day views, each day's whole tree or branch with ancestors as context.
- **Shared pieces** (`components/todos`): `TodoRow` (moved from the Todos route; one row with slight looks per place: list, card for the Planner's top row, easy, and a dimmed context look for an ancestor; `PlannerTodoRow` and the Easy card's row fold into it), `TodoTree` (nested rows, indented per level, an optional quick add per row), `TodoQuickAdd` (compact and full forms over `createTodo`; Enter adds and keeps the field; errors inline; writers only, by `canHaveSubtask`).
- **The Todos page** renders sections of trees; the Done list stays flat and leaves out a done step whose top-level todo is open. **The Planner** and the Easy view's Upcoming render day trees; **the Easy view's My todos** renders my trees; **the Today card** names the due step. **The todo page** renders `TodoTree` of its children under the band's progress line, with `TodoQuickAdd` (full) for new children. The tick, Undo and "Part of" up-link work as today.
- No migration, no sync rule change, no API contract change.

## Testing decisions

- **Unit:** the tree rules (`clampDue`, `inheritedDue`, `siblingOrder`, `upcomingListsSubtask`), the tree view (placement, filters, order, day trees), `todoList`, `plannerTodos`, `todoStrip`. Prior art: `todoTree.test.ts`, `todoList.test.ts`.
- **Integration:** `todoService` create inherits and clamps, update clamps a child, a parent moved earlier or given a first day clamps open descendants only, an upload past the parent is clamped not refused; the feed and the Google push leave subtasks out and withdraw a held subtask event. Prior art: the todo-subtasks service tests.
- **Component (browser):** the Planner's day trees, the Easy view's trees, the Todos page's trees (levels, done children, Overdue placement, Mine), the row + (adds, stays open, absent for viewers and at max depth), the todo page's tree and add field; the device's inherit, refuse and cascade in `todoSubtasks.sync.browser.test.tsx`. No new end-to-end check: `roundtrip` already covers todo rows offline.

## Out of scope

- Collapsing a tree; dragging to reorder or re-parent; moving a subtask to another parent.
- Quick add from timeline entries and linkable pages (B-948, todo-links), which reuses `TodoQuickAdd`.
- The shared displays audit (B-961).
- A view option to split trees into individual due todos (B-964).
