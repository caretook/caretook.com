# Data-copy register

Every place a copy of care data (or of the people it's about) lives once Caretook is hosted, and the rules for each: the security review's S-6 (`plans/security-review.md`). It answers the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule's questions: what we hold, where, who can reach it, and how a vendor tells us about a breach. The legal pages (B-54) promise only what this register says.

- **Care data** here means anything about a care recipient or their care: timeline entries, notes, vitals, medications, providers, care periods, allergies, profiles. **People data** means account details: names, email addresses, roles.
- **To confirm** cells are filled in when going live (hosting slice 7) from the vendor's dashboard, plan or DPA, with the link. Until then the legal pages must not promise anything those cells would decide.
- Staging holds only the fictional seed and the team's own accounts; its rows are at the end.
- Update this file whenever a copy is added (a new vendor, a log drain, an AI provider), a setting changes, or a cell is confirmed.

## What has to be true for each promise

- **"Encrypted at rest"** holds only once the Neon, PowerSync and Resend cells are confirmed. Devices aren't encrypted by us.
- **"Staff see care data only with permission, recorded"** holds for Postgres through `break-glass.md`'s roles and audit entries. It doesn't yet cover the Neon console, the PowerSync dashboard or the Resend dashboard, which are limited to named people but not recorded by us; and Neon's own statement logging is **to confirm**.
- **"Deleted when you ask"** doesn't hold yet: soft deletes keep content in Postgres, history, PowerSync and devices (S-1, the `data-rights` PRD).
